#+TITLE: R24 — walltime + parallelism efficiency analysis (PARALLELISM=32)
#+FILETAGS: :wiki:multi-agent-org-llm:bench:duration:r24:
#+OPTIONS: toc:1 num:nil

* Summary

R24 ran *147 cells in 54.3 min wall* (3260s) at *32-way parallel* —
*nearly 2× R18's wall* despite only +5% more cells. Doubling workers
16 → 32 did not help: the round was *Layer-1-bound*, where a single
=K2-kimi-k2.6= cell ran *1612s (27 min)* and held the layer open while
~110 other workers idled. Effective parallelism across the round is
*7.81×* (24% of the 32-way ceiling) — better than R18's 16% but the
absolute wall went up because the long-tail variants (K2-kimi, K7-qwen72b)
got both bigger samples (n=20 + n=10) /and/ no enforcement of
=WALL_CAP_PER_CELL_S=400=. The new Layer-BK ran cleanly in *157s*
(2.6 min for 10 long-horizon cells); BK is /not/ the wall problem —
Layer 1 is. R25 with Sp1+Sp2+Sp3+Sp4 enforced should land at
*22-28 min* for ~470 cells (5× the cells, half the wall).

Note on the "~22-min process elapsed" estimate in the brief: actual
wallclock from log timestamps is *54.3 min* (08:19:42 → 09:14:02).
The 22-min figure may be a stale snapshot or a single phase; this
analysis uses log-derived numbers throughout.

* 1. Total wall vs projection

| Round  | Cells | Workers | Wall (min) | Wall (s) | Cells/min | Long-pole cell |
|--------+-------+---------+------------+----------+-----------+----------------|
| R17    |    59 |       8 |       20.0 |     1200 |       3.0 | K7 ablations   |
| R18 v2 |   140 |      16 |       28.1 |     1687 |      1.78 | K5/B5 = 648s   |
| R24    |   147 |      32 |       54.3 |     3260 |      2.71 | K2/B1 = 1612s  |

R18 → R24: workers 2×, cells +5%, wall *1.93×*. Why "more workers"
didn't help: (1) Layer 1 was scaled up massively (115 cells on B1 vs
~50 in R18); (2) longest cell got worse (1612s vs 648s — 2.5× ceiling
regression with =WALL_CAP_PER_CELL_S= unenforced); (3) sum-of-walls
grew 7.4× (30,009s vs 4,333s) while workers grew only 2×.

*Summary.* Median 160s, p90 311s, p95 674s, p99 1068s, max 1612s.
The distribution is *much wider than R18* on every percentile above
p50 — long-tail dilation, not a uniform shift.

| Layer    |   n |  Sum-walls | Max cell | Floor | Actual | Eff par |
|----------+-----+------------+----------+-------+--------+---------|
| 1        | 115 |     26,686 |     1612 |  1612 |   2097 |  12.73× |
| 2        |   8 |        991 |      466 |   466 |    466 |   2.13× |
| K11-B25  |   8 |        217 |       33 |    33 |     33 |   6.58× |
| 3        |   6 |       1339 |      444 |   444 |    444 |   3.02× |
| BK       |  10 |        776 |      157 |   157 |    157 |   4.94× |
| /Total/  | 147 |   /30,009/ |     1612 |  /-/  |  /3197/| /9.39×/ |

Round-level eff par: *9.39× of 32 = 29.3% of ceiling* (vs R18's 16%).

*Layer 1 is the bottleneck.* 115 cells stuffed into one layer for
per-variant statistics, but the scheduler is max-cell-bound. Even at
perfect 32-way the floor is the longest cell (1612s). Of L1's 2097s
wall, only 1612s could be parallelized — the remaining 485s is *idle
worker time* with one cell running alone. Layers 2/3 had ≤8 cells
so eff_par > 2.5× is structurally impossible there; they're already
at theoretical ceiling.

Pre-Layer-1 startup is 63s (eager prefetch + warmup, comparable to
R18's 60s). All inter-layer barriers are ≤2s. Pure barrier overhead
is essentially zero (≤10s round-total). The *real* "wasted" wall is
single-cell long-tails: ~1300s (22 min) of the 54-min wall is
one-cell-holds-layer-open time.

* 6. Layer-BK walltime characteristics

*Summary.* BK ran 10 cells (5 tasks × 2 variants) in 157s wall —
under 3 min for the full long-horizon battery. None of the BK cells
became wall-pole; the BK layer is *not* an R24 wall problem.

*Expanded.* BK = K2-kimi-k2.6 + K5-claude-solo × BK1-BK5. Per-cell
walls (sorted desc): 157, 126, 121, 115, 60, 56, 55, 34, 33, 19.
Median 58s, max 157s, sum 776s. The max-cell (157s K2 on last task)
defines layer wall. Task ↔ completion mapping isn't recoverable from
log-only data (summary doesn't preserve order for FOSS cells), but
the 5 BK tasks land in the 19-157s band — none is dominant.

*BK is much faster than R18's projection* (R18 expected p50 ~600s,
p90 ~1500s). Either BK fixtures are softer than estimated, or
=brief_mode=tight= bounds the long-horizon output. Either way, BK is
*not* an R25 wall risk.

* 7. R25 wall projection — Sp1-Sp7 ranked by implied savings

R25 design carries 7 stack speed-ups (Sp1-Sp7). Ranked by R24-actual
wall savings:

| Rank | Stack | Mechanism                      | R24 wall hit | Implied save | Confidence |
|------+-------+--------------------------------+--------------+--------------+------------|
| 1    | Sp4   | Drop K7-qwen72b                | 5594s sum    | -930s wall   | high       |
| 2    | Sp3   | Self-host K1+K2 endpoints      | 11,014s sum  | -680s wall   | high       |
| 3    | Sp2   | Pipeline L1 → L2               | 485s idle    | -485s wall   | medium     |
| 4    | Sp5   | S33 speculative on long-output | 0s (BK soft) | -0 to -300s  | low (BK)   |
| 5    | Sp1   | Skip Layer 3 dial ablation     | 444s wall    | -444s wall   | high       |
| 6    | Sp6   | S34 async pipeline on S3 cells | n/a (R25)    | -180-300s    | medium     |
| 7    | Sp7   | Drop B7/B25 from L1 sample-up  | n/a (R25)    | -120s wall   | high       |

Ranking notes:

1. *Sp4 (drop K7) wins*. K7's 10 cells summed to 5594s wall with 5
   cells over 700s; K7 owned 5/10 of the round's worst cells. Wall
   save derived from K7-cells-as-Layer-1-long-poles: ~930s. K7
   quality is also dead (-270 score). High confidence.
2. *Sp3 (self-host K1+K2)*: K2 alone accounted for 9663s (32% of
   sum-walls) including the 1612s outlier. vLLM prefix-cache TTFT
   would drop K2 median 175s → ~50s; outlier cells likely stop too
   (no cloud-broker stochasticity). 680s save is conservative.
3. *Sp2 (pipeline L1 → L2)*: directly recovers the 485s of idle
   worker time at end of L1 while one K2 cell ran alone.
4. *Sp1 (skip Layer 3)*: full L3 wall = 444s. Dial ablation has
   converged after 6 rounds.
5. *Sp5 (S33 speculative)*: BK cells were already short in R24
   (max 157s) so 40-50% reduction buys little; benefit only realized
   on harder R25 BK fixtures.
6. *Sp6 (S34 async)*: R25-only, no R24 baseline. ~12 S3 cells × 50%
   = -180-300s.
7. *Sp7 (drop B7/B25 from L1)*: R24 didn't run B7/B25 in L1; design
   quote ~120s.

*Combined R25 wall projection* (with all Sp1-Sp7 enforced + the
hard =WALL_CAP_PER_CELL_S=400= turned on):

| Scenario                              | R25 wall   | Notes                        |
|---------------------------------------+------------+------------------------------|
| R24-style execution at 470 cells      | ~165 min   | Linear scale-up (untenable)  |
| + Sp1-Sp7 stacks, no wall-cap         | 45-55 min  | R25-design quote             |
| + Sp1-Sp7 stacks + =wall_cap=400=     | *25-35 min*| Kills 13 outlier cells outright |
| + skip K7 entirely + self-host K1/K2  | *22-28 min*| Recommended R25 floor        |
| Hedged retry on cells >300s p90       | *18-25 min*| Aspirational                 |

*Headline*: R25 with Sp4 + Sp3 + Sp2 + =WALL_CAP_PER_CELL_S=400=
/enforced/ should deliver *22-28 min wall for ~470 cells* — 5× R24's
cells in half the wall. Leverage is on long-tail variants (K2
outliers + K7 generally), not on adding more workers.

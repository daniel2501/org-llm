* 4. Did extended quality_lint produce false positives now?

Mostly fixed; one bug remains.

** What R25's fix accomplished

=count_nested_bracket_depth= scans per-line, depth resets at line
boundaries. K1's 14 R24 false-positive =nested_brackets=2= cells
now report 0. R24's recommended FP-1 patch landed.

** What still bites: =count_bracket_errors= cross-line cascade

R25 patched =count_nested_bracket_depth= but left
=count_bracket_errors= walking all added text in one pass. When a
diff edits a wiki-link with one missing =]= on a =+=-line whose
closing context is ten lines later, the walker treats the unclosed
=[[= as still-open and counts every subsequent well-formed
=[[id:UUID][label]]= as a new "nested" event. One-real / N-reported:

| cell                          | reported | true | inflation |
|-------------------------------+----------+------+-----------|
| K11 s4 B1                     |       17 |    1 |       16x |
| K11 s2 B1                     |        8 |    1 |        8x |
| K11 s1 B1                     |        2 |    1 |        2x |
| K17 s1-s3 B1                  |        6 |    1 |        6x |
| K17 s4 B1                     |       10 |    1 |       10x |
| K8 s4-s13 B1 (6 replicates)   |        6 |    1 |        6x |

Six K8 cells share byte-identical 6.0 — same prefetch across seeds,
all 1 real broken line. K11 s4 amplifies to 17 because its diff has
20 added bracket-link lines. K8 also legit fires =nested_brackets=1=
(=[[*TODO [#A] Planned — [[id:...]]]= is a real depth-2 nest), so
real_lint=2.0 there, not 1.0.

** Concrete R26 fix

Mirror the per-line walker into =count_bracket_errors=: count
=imbalance= and =nested= line-by-line, sum across lines. Single-file
patch to =scripts/quality_lint.py=. Validation: re-run on R25 saved
diffs; K7-style intra-line cascades still fire; K11/K17/K8 cross-
line cascades collapse to 1.0/cell.

* 7. R26 advisory

** R-1: Patch =count_bracket_errors= per-line, same as =count_nested_bracket_depth=

Single-file change to =scripts/quality_lint.py=. Walker counts
imbalance + nested per-line, sums across lines. Validation:
re-run R25 saved diffs; K11/K17/K8 collapse to 1.0/cell; K1 clean
stays 0; K7-style intra-line cascades unchanged. Removes 4 false
floor-drops; unblocks G1 for real defects.

** R-2: Fix top-3 selector to pick variants, not cells

Layer-1's =top3_foss = [c["variant"] for c in foss_cells[:3]]=
picks 3 individual *cells* and dedups by accident. In R25 all 3 were
K1 (=top3_foss_after_layer1: ["K1-qwen30", "K1-qwen30", "K1-qwen30"]=
in summary), so Layer-2+3+BK collapsed to K1+Claude+force-list.
K2's 32 successful cells (15.19 adj/cell) never advanced. Fix:
aggregate by variant first, rank by per-cell mean (not sum, so
wall-cap-kills don't dilute), take top-3 distinct variants. With
this, R25's top-3 would have been K2 (15.19), K17 (16.80), K1
(8.41) — diverse pool.

** R-3: Raise K2 B1 wall cap from 400s to 600s

K2's mean wall on successful B1 is 200-280s; 400s killed 16 of 50.
K1/K8 finish <120s — only K2 hits this cap hard. Bump to 600s for
K2 (or globally); expect +~25 K2 cells at <$1 incremental.
Alternative: =wall_cap_kill=warn=.

** R-4: Layer-BK on full advancing pool, not just top-3

Currently Layer-BK = top-3 FOSS ∪ Claude. With R-2 that's
K2+K17+K1+K5 — still missing K8/K11. R26 should run BK1-BK5 across
every variant that cleared Layer-1 score>0. Per-cell cost ~$0.02
FOSS / $0.20 Claude; K8+K11+K17 × 5 BK ≈ $2-4 incremental. Cheap
diversity signal — BK is the only long-horizon family.

** R-5: Force K8 + K11 onto non-B1 tasks before promotion

K8's 28 cells are all B1. R24 advised "test K8 on non-B1 before
promotion"; R25 force-listed K8 to Layer-2 but its 4 Layer-2 cells
all silent_noop'd (B5, B7, B11, B25). That's not recovery — it's
confirmation K8 doesn't generalize off B1. K11's 8 layer_k11b25
were the only working diversity test. R26: K8 on B11+B26 (n=5 each),
K11 on B5/B7/B11 (n=5 each); hold K8/K11 promotion decisions until
non-B1 data exists.

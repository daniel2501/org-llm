* Summary

*Summary.* Across R15-R19 (359 ~cell_result.json~ on disk), 20 distinct
failure classes appeared. Three are *load-bearing*: bracket_imbalance
(53 cells, every round), silent_noop (38 cells, every round), and
worktree_race (30 cells, R17+R18 only — R18 was the explosion).
broker_tool_format_gap is the newest hard-pin failure (first
auto-detectable in R18 v1 K8-deepseekV3, fixed in v2 by
~allow_fallbacks=True~, regressed in R19 K8 with 5 reproductions).
fab_uuid is small (13 cells) but high-impact when it lands. R20+ needs
five guards: pre-create worktrees serially, mech penalty for bracket /
fab / unreachable code, ~provider.ignore~ deny-list, and a per-task
~expected_diff_floor~ to reward correct abstain.

*Expanded.* This is a count-based taxonomy. Counts come from a
deterministic scan of every ~cell_result.json~ under
~scripts/_round{15..19}_dials_artifacts/~ (including R18's ~v2/~
subtree), classified by error string, ~analysis~ block, edit-arg
regex, and ~text_output~ heuristics. Cells that never reached the
broker (silent harness skips that produced no JSON) are NOT counted —
the live log notes such cases for R18 v1 (10 silent K7/K8/K17/K15/K5
variants) and R18 v2 worktree-add concurrency that may have killed up
to 90 cells without writing JSON. So infra-failure rates here are
*lower bounds*. The classification rules are documented inline in
~/tmp/scan_failure_modes.py~ (kept local; the report is the artifact).

Notes:
- *destructive_edit, malformed_output (org/elisp/python validity beyond
  brackets), under_count, over_count, append_only_violation* all need
  ground-truth from the brief or post-edit AST/parse — they exist in
  the runs (the R18 v2 quality-judge note reproduces ≥6 of them) but
  are *not* in cell_result.json. R20 should ship ~scripts/quality_lint.py~.
- *broker_tool_format_gap* in R18: the most catastrophic case was R18
  v1 K8-deepseekV3 (live-log: ~Here are the function calls...~ then
  =stop=, 0 tool_calls, KILLED at 02:00 EDT after 5 min / 96 cells).
  That cell did *not* write a cell_result.json — the harness aborted
  the run. R18 v2 K2-kimi-k2.6 on DeepInfra wrote 27,013 chars of
  analysis text with finish_reason=length / 0 tool_calls and IS
  counted (under runaway_reasoning).
- *worktree_race* in R18: 29 cell_result.json rows on disk show the
  =git worktree add ... returned non-zero exit status 255= signature
  (10 in v2, 19 in v3). The R18 quality-judge note estimates *up to 90
  total* cells died here, which would mean ~60 never wrote JSON.

* 5. Infrastructure-failure rate vs model-behavior rate per round

A cell is *infra-failed* if it has any of {max_iter_reached, http_402,
http_5xx, plan_parse_failed, runtime_exception, tcp_drop, worktree_race,
worktree_collision, broker_tool_format_gap}; *model-failed* if any of
{silent_noop, runaway_reasoning, scope_violation, tool_misuse,
fab_uuid, bracket_imbalance}. A cell can be both. Rates use ~n_cells~
on disk (lower bound — cells that never wrote JSON not counted).

| round | n_cells | infra-fail cells | model-fail cells | infra rate | model rate | both |
|-------+---------+------------------+------------------+------------+------------+------|
| R15   |      49 |                5 |                8 |      10.2% |      16.3% |    1 |
| R16   |      54 |                4 |               14 |       7.4% |      25.9% |    2 |
| R17   |      55 |                5 |               16 |       9.1% |      29.1% |    1 |
| R18   |     128 |                5 |               36 |       3.9% |      28.1% |    1 |
| R19   |      73 |               23 |               29 |      31.5% |      39.7% |   12 |

*Reading R18 carefully.* The on-disk infra-rate of 3.9% looks
deceptively low because the 90-ish R18 v2 worktree-fail cells from the
quality-judge audit don't appear in cell_result.json; they show up in
the live log as missing variants. If we imputed those (90 worktree +
the 19 v3 worktree on-disk + 10 v2 on-disk), R18 infra-rate is ~70%
of the run, dwarfing the 28% model-rate. *R18 was an
infrastructure-bound round*, not a model-quality round.

*R19 is the alarm.* Infra-rate 31.5% on disk is the highest in the
arc, driven by max_iter_reached (11) + tcp_drop (5) + broker_tool_format_gap
(5 K8-deepseekV3 cells where the v1 issue regressed). R19 also has the
highest model-rate (39.7%), driven mostly by bracket_imbalance (16 of
73 = 22%). The two together mean *only ~30% of R19 cells were
clean-infra+clean-model* — leaderboard trust on R19 is correspondingly
low without the quality_lint pass.

*Trend.* Model-rate has been climbing R15→R19 (16% → 26% → 29% → 28%
→ 40%) because the variant pool expanded (K10-K19 added in R16-R17,
then more reasoning models in R18) and the brief got tighter in R17+,
exposing more bracket / fab-UUID failures in tail-quality models.
Infra-rate is volatile — R17 stable, R18 hidden-bad, R19 visible-bad —
so guards #1 (worktree mutex) and #3 (provider deny-list) plus #6
(first-iter tool_call gate) are the highest-leverage shipped-before-R20
items.

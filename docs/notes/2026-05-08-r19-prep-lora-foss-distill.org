* 6 — Pre-committed A/B bar (restated for clarity)

Per the LoRA-prep agent's gating output (R18 distill-dataset stats):

- *Display-text quality* (Wiki Rule 2b summaries, DEC-entry shape,
  captains-log entries, free-form 2-paragraph wiki notes — see
  =EVAL_PROBES= in =02_train.py=):

  K20 must score *at least +3 points higher* than the strongest
  baseline-FOSS teacher (K11-qwen3coder is the natural baseline since
  K20's base = K11's serving model).

- *Edit-accuracy* (B1/B5/B11/B25/B26 worktree tasks, judged by the
  same R15-R17 quality rubric):

  K20 may NOT regress more than 5 points below K11 baseline. Distill
  is allowed to specialize toward voice; it must not break the survey-
  before-edit rhythm that the corpus encodes.

- *Judge*: blinded Opus rater (per the validate-orchestration-with-AB
  feedback rule); reuse =scripts/recipe_ab_harness.py= +
  =scripts/recipe_ab_judge.py= as the harness template.

- *Decision rule*:
  - Both bars cleared → adopt K20 in R18 v3 / R19 variant pool.
  - +3pt display but >5pt edit regression → keep K20 as a /narration/
    role only (not a /worktree-edit/ role).
  - <+3pt display → discard the run; revisit dataset (more cells,
    higher quality floor, different LoRA rank).

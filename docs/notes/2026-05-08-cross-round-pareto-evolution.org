* Summary

*Summary.* Five rounds traced a clean FOSS Pareto march: K1-qwen30
went from $0.0005/u (R15, n=14) to *$0.00047/u (R19, n=28)* while
K5-claude-solo drifted up $0.0106 -> *$0.0242/u*. *FOSS-vs-Claude $/u
gap widened from 21x to 51x.* K11/K9/K7 dominated multiple times; K2
fell off (R18 broker) and recovered big (R19).

*Expanded.* Two structural shifts: (1) Claude's $/u drifted *up* every
round (longer prose, more tool calls), eroding the FOSS bar; (2) FOSS
$/u held or improved as tasks got harder (R19 added long-horizon
BK1-BK5), implying real per-token efficiency. K11-qwen3coder is the
standing case study in "premium FOSS that never earned its premium" --
dominated R17 (35x K1, +14% mech), R18 (40x K1, equal), R19 (22x K1).

* Section 2 -- Frontier evolution (FOSS only)

Each round's Pareto-optimal FOSS set (cost <= AND score >= than every
non-frontier variant). K5-claude-solo informational; never on FOSS
frontier per project rule.

| round | frontier set                | cheapest $/u | best $/u verdict        |
|-------+-----------------------------+--------------+-------------------------|
| R15   | K1, K8                      | K8 0.0008    | K1 0.0005 (best total)  |
| R16   | K1, K8, K6 (n=1), K7        | K6 0.0006    | K1 0.0006               |
| R17   | K1, K8                      | K8 0.0005    | K1 0.0008 (n=14 stable) |
| R18v2 | K1, K6                      | K1 0.0010    | K1 = K6 (tie)           |
| R19   | K1, K7 (mid-tier), K8 (low) | K1 0.00047   | *K1 0.00047 (best ever)* |

*Frontier shifted 3x.* R15->R16 gained K7 (premium tier). R17: K7
worktree-errored, K8 re-emerged. R18: K8 broker-broke, K6 climbed on.
R19: K8 regressed, K1 absorbed all headroom -- frontier collapsed to
one dominant variant (K1) with K7+K8 mid-tier.

** Headline surprises

1. *K5-claude per-cell tripled R18->R19* ($0.101 -> $0.370). Long-horizon
   BK1-BK5 ballooned Claude's per-task token spend -- mechanism for the
   $0.0242/u bar.
2. *K17-glm46 cell cost rose 20x* R18->R19 ($0.004 -> $0.082) *but score
   rose 28x* (8 -> 222) -- $/u *improved* 0.0049 -> 0.0037. Quiet climber.
3. *K11 per-cell ranged 12x* across rounds ($0.020-$0.243). Highest-variance
   variant; never earned its premium any round.
4. *K6 stayed cheap forever* ($0.002-0.009/cell) but score collapsed R19
   (59 -> 18) -- cheapest in panel, unstable on long-horizon tasks.
5. *K1's per-cell rose R18->R19* (0.0021 -> 0.010) yet $/u *improved*
   (0.0010 -> 0.00047): score rose 13x while cost rose 5x -- absorbed
   harder tasks efficiently.
6. *K8 broker bug R18 hid a real R19 regression.* R17 frontier 0.0005/u,
   R18 broken, R19 0.0017/u (3x worse than R17). Broken round masked
   the loss-of-frontier story.
7. *Round total trend.* $1.62 -> $2.07 -> $4.45 -> $2.77 -> $9.75. R17
   was a "premium variants tested" peak; R19 is the first round where
   *intentional spend* (n=28 K1 + long-horizon) drove totals, not
   error-replays.

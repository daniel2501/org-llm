* Summary

*Summary.* Mined 562 cells across R15, R16, R17, R18 (v1+v2), and R19
to find the best (variant, dial, prompt-shape) combination per task
shape. The dominant dial =bt_pb_m1_tb_s1= (tight brief / both-prefetch
/ manager-on / tool-broad / scope-strict) ran 496 of 562 cells — its
ablations (~11 cells each) carry weak signal for most tasks but
suggest =bt_pi_m1_tb_s1= (inline-only prefetch) is the B1 winner.
Per-task winners separate cleanly: K1-qwen30 dominates wiki-edit
volume + cost; K2-kimi-k2.6 is the small-atomic frontier; K5-claude
holds DEC-draft (B11) by 8 quality units but is FOSS-banned; K7-qwen72b
is the only consistent code-edit (B7) winner; K11-qwen3coder takes
elisp (B25) at 20 vs runner-up 12 but on n=1. BK1-BK5 long-horizon
fixtures are wired but unmeasured — R20 first samples.

*Expanded.* This doc collapses the leaderboard view from R15-R19 down
to a routing decision per task family. The §8 Python literal drops
directly into =scripts/_round20_dials.py= as =TASK_FAMILY_VARIANTS=.
Methodology: aggregate every =cell_result.json= via the per-round
=summary-*.json= rollups, group by (task, variant, dial), rank by
mean score with sample-count tiebreaker, then collapse tasks into
families. Caveats: tied-zero floors (B13/B20/B26) carry no signal;
B11 has only one cell per FOSS variant; dial ablations only ran on
B1 — every per-task "best dial" outside B1 is =bt_pb_m1_tb_s1= by
construction.

* Top

*Summary.* This supplement extends the in-flight R15-R19 cross-round
analyses with the four overnight rounds R20-R23. R20 produced one
genuinely new datum — K2-kimi-k2.6 reaches K1-qwen30 parity on quality
once given n=25 (488 vs 473), but K1's $/qpt advantage is preserved at
7.2x. R21-R23 hit OpenRouter HTTP 402 at 04:39 EDT and yielded zero
FOSS data; only the Anthropic-billed K5-claude-solo scored. The
practical takeaway is that R20 alone earns its keep, and R21-R23 only
become useful after the credit watcher re-runs them post-top-up.

*Expanded.* All four rounds ran the same fixed-point dial config
(=bt_pb_m1_tb_s1=, the R19 winner) so they are sample-size additions,
not strategy probes. The intended R19 BK1-BK5 long-horizon tasks did
NOT enter the layer-1 cell pool in R20-R23 — every layer-1 cell is B1,
with B5/B7/B11/B13/B20/B25/B26 only appearing once each in R20 layer-2
re-runs. The 402-cap obliterated R21-R23 FOSS variance signal; layer-2
promotion (the dead-variant prune) never fired in R21-R23 because no
FOSS variant produced a layer-1 score to promote on. K5-claude-solo
showed surprisingly tight consistency across R21/R22/R23 (74/93/93 with
identical primary=92/93/93), making it the only legitimate
cross-round repeatability datum produced by these rounds.

* 2. Pareto evolution R20-R23

| Round | Pareto front (FOSS)                                      | $/qpt frontier              |
|-------+----------------------------------------------------------+-----------------------------|
| R19   | K1 (lowest cost-per-unit), K11 (highest single score)    | K1 0.00047 / K7 0.00159     |
| R20   | K1 (cheapest), K2 (highest single score)                 | K1 0.00045 / K8 0.00092     |
| R21   | K5-only (no FOSS data; 402)                              | n/a                         |
| R22   | K5-only (no FOSS data; 402)                              | n/a                         |
| R23   | K5-only (no FOSS data; 402)                              | n/a                         |

K1 still owns the cost-per-unit frontier: R19 0.00047, R20 0.00045 —
a 4% improvement, well within noise. K2-kimi at $1.59 / 488 = 0.00326
$/qpt is *6.7x* more expensive per quality unit than K1, but earned
the highest single-variant total in any round. The Pareto answer:
*K1 still dominates if you optimize $/qpt; K2 dominates if you
optimize raw quality at a fixed n.* These are the same two operating
points the R15-R19 pareto-evolution agent was already separating —
R20 sharpens the wedge but does not change the slope.

K8-deepseekV3 surfaced a new low-cost frontier point ($0.00092/qpt at
n=25, score 124) that didn't exist in R19 (where it was at n=15 with
score 38). This is the one structurally new R20 datum for Pareto.

Outside of 402, R20 is remarkably clean. The =safe_worktree_add= retry
fix from commit fc59d1c held — only one git-worktree error in 140
cells. No broker errors and no scope_violation rejections appeared in
the cell records. The R19 BK1-BK5 long-horizon tasks did NOT trigger
any new failure modes because they were not actually exercised at
layer 1 — the harness defaulted to B1 for the bulk of cells.

The 402 distribution leaks the spend pattern: R20 already had K11
(5 of 10 cells), K17 (7 of 10), K15 (3 of 5) and K1 (12 of 28) hitting
402 before the round closed. K11-qwen3coder is the credit-killer — at
$0.03296/qpt it consumed 5x more per unit than any other priced FOSS
variant.

* 5. Best-strategy-per-shape R20-R23

the R20 layer-2 expansion only ran K1-qwen30 + K5-claude-solo on the
seven other tasks (B5/B7/B11/B13/B20/B25/B26). The R19 BK1-BK5
long-horizon tasks were NOT exercised — neither in layer 1 nor in any

* 7. Strategy effectiveness R20-R23

*R20 delta — n=25 bump for K2 + K8.* Did surfacing more cells reveal
new variance signal? Mixed. K2 went 15→25 cells and gained 160 score
points (10.7/cell average) — confirmed it can score, but the variance
is still huge (max 38, min 0, mean 19.5/cell on B1). K8 went 15→25
cells and gained 86 points (3.4/cell mean) — at 25 cells K8 is now a
clearly weaker variant than K1/K2. The bump *did* surface variance
but did NOT change the variant ranking.

*R21 delta — claimed dead-variant auto-prune.* Inspecting the harness
diffs (R20→R21 is identical except round-id renames, lines 71/75/460/
604/610/1102/1177/1280/1381/1616/1717), there is no R21-introduced
prune logic — the layer-1 → layer-2 dead-variant filter has been
present since R17 (=Layer 1 → advancing (after dead-variant filter)=).
Whatever R21 was supposed to test never had the chance to run because
all FOSS variants 402-failed at layer 1, leaving the prune filter with
empty input. So this delta cannot be evaluated from these artifacts.

*Net.* The fixed-point config is now thoroughly proven (R20 best_config
identical to R19) but no R20-R23 round actually probed dial space —
all 521 cells across 4 rounds used =bt_pb_m1_tb_s1=. The bench has
plateaued on strategy and is now in pure sample-size accumulation
mode. R24 should reintroduce dial variance OR introduce BK1-BK5 to
get new signal.

* 8. Master synthesis

Combining R20-R23 with the headline R15-R19 narrative
(=docs/wiki/2026-05-08-r18-synthesis.org=) and the in-flight
cross-round reports
(=docs/notes/2026-05-08-cross-round-{quality-trend,pareto-evolution,
failure-modes,variant-longevity,best-strategy-per-shape,outlier-
deepdive}.org=, may not exist yet at read time):

1. *K1-qwen30's $/qpt frontier is now confirmed across two
   independent rounds* (R19: 0.00047, R20: 0.00045). The
   FOSS-spend-headroom thesis is empirically grounded.

2. *K2-kimi-k2.6 is a peer at n=25 but not at $/qpt.* The R19
   pareto-evolution report should pin K1+K2 as the two surviving
   FOSS frontier points; everyone else is dominated.

3. *The fixed-point dial config is stable.* R19's
   =bt_pb_m1_tb_s1= won R20 with the same best_config; R21-R23 used
   it without modification. This is a real plateau — strategy
   variance is no longer where the wins are.

4. *BK1-BK5 are unfired chambers.* The R19 long-horizon-tasks
   wiring landed (fixtures imported) but the R20-R23 harness never
   selected them. R24 must schedule them explicitly into layer 1.

5. *K11/K15/K17 are credit-cliff variants.* Their per-cell cost
   inside a $30 cap is the round-killer; K11 alone consumed enough
   to trigger the 402 cap at 04:39. Move them to a separate pool
   with its own budget cap or drop.

6. *K5-claude-solo's R21/R22/R23 consistency (74/93/93 with primary
   92/93/93) is the cleanest cross-round repeatability datum we have.*
   Use it as the calibration anchor for any future judge-LLM
   reliability work.

7. *R21-R23 forked harnesses are still on disk and ready to re-run.*
   Now that OpenRouter is at $90 / $59 headroom (post 11:30 EDT
   top-up), the credit watcher can run the three forked harnesses
   without modification — they'll produce the dial-variance data the
   first overnight pass missed.

8. *Failure-mode taxonomy is essentially "402 plus everything else".*
   Outside of payment errors, R20-R23 had 3 IncompleteReads, 1
   git-worktree exit-128, 2 silent_noops, and ~40 score-0
   non-empty-diff cells. The non-402 surface is now tractable.

* Summary

*Summary.* R24 ran 147 cells / *$13.29 spent* / 53 min wall across
8 variants; layer-1 absorbed 132 cells (mostly B1), layer-2 + layer_bk
fired only on K2 and K5. Three errors only -- the worktree-retry fix
held. *K1-qwen30 is back on the $/qpt frontier at $0.00054/qpt* (R20
0.00045, R19 0.00047 -- all within noise); *K8-deepseekV3 climbed
back on at $0.00090/qpt* after R20's regression. K11 is dominated
again (44x K1). K7 produced the round's most striking failure: lint
penalty avalanche drove *score=-693* across 10 cells, the first
strictly-negative-aggregate variant in the bench history.

*Expanded.* The frontier has three FOSS points this round (K1 cheapest
$/qpt, K8 second-cheapest at half K1's absolute spend, K6 cheapest
absolute cell at $0.0055) versus R19/R20's two-point frontier. K2
keeps the highest absolute score (667) but at *8.5x K1's $/qpt* --
peer on quality, dominated on efficiency. K17 climbed back ($0.00239
/qpt, n=10/259 score) after R20's 402-blackout, sitting just off the
frontier. K11's $4.39 single-variant spend was the biggest cost
surprise; K2's $3.05 (mostly B1, n=30) and K5's $4.03 (full task
matrix) round out the top-3 spend. The bench now has *5 rounds* of
K1 $/qpt readings clustered in [0.00045, 0.00078]; no other variant
has that stability.

** Headline surprises

1. *K2 spent $3.05 vs ~$1.00 R20 projection.* Driver: 30 layer-1 B1
   cells at high n + 4 layer-2 cells + 5 layer_bk cells across BK1-BK5.
   The layer_bk cells averaged $0.10 each and produced *negative
   aggregate score* (-8 across 5 cells, lint penalties on long-horizon
   tasks). Recommend reducing K2 n=40 -> n=15 in R25 -- the variance
   is not paying for itself at this depth.

2. *K11 spent $4.39 -- biggest single-variant spend in R24* and the
   highest single-variant spend across all bench rounds (vs R19 $2.43,
   R20 $2.40 stub). 10 B1 cells at $0.36 each + 8 B25 cells at $0.09
   each. Per-cell on B1 is now *2x R19's $0.18*. The credit-killer
   variant from the R20-R23 supplement is still credit-killing.

3. *K8 recovered.* R20 $0.00092/qpt with score 124 -> R24 $0.00090/qpt
   with score 164 at the same n=25. The R19 score=38 regression (per
   R20 supplement) was a transient; K8 is structurally on the frontier
   when it runs cleanly. *K8 + K6 reclaim the second-tier frontier
   position that K1 absorbed in R19.*

4. *K7 score=-693 is the round's biggest anomaly.* All 10 cells ran
   without harness errors. Primary score 19-28 (in the K1/K8 zone)
   gated by lint penalties of 25-136 driven by *bracket_errors and
   nested_brackets compounding*. This is the first time the lint
   subsystem produced a strictly-negative aggregate at production n.
   Either K7's tokenizer regressed, the lint rule regressed, or both.

5. *K5 cost tripled vs R20.* $0.0822/cell -> $0.28795/cell. R20-R23
   K5 ran B1-only; R24 K5 ran the full 8-task matrix including layer_bk
   long-horizon at $0.10-0.34 per cell. This is the FIRST cross-round
   read of Claude on BK1-BK5 -- and Claude's negative scores on the
   layer_bk cells (-8 aggregate) match K2's pattern, suggesting the
   long-horizon tasks penalize *all* solvers at low n.

6. *Total round spend $13.29 vs ~$5 projected.* 2.6x over. Drivers: K11
   (35% of budget), K5 (30%), K2 (23%). The three frontier variants
   (K1+K8+K6) consumed only $0.43 combined -- *3.2% of round spend
   delivered 53% of total scoring volume*. R25 must rebalance.

* Section 7 -- R25 advisory (concrete recommendations)

1. *Lock K1+K8+K6 as the R25 default frontier* (n=28+25+15 ~ $0.75).
   Three rounds of evidence (R19+R20+R24) show K1's $/qpt is the bench
   floor; K8/K6 fill the second/cheapest-cell slots cleanly.

2. *Cap K2 at n=15 single-task* (not R24's n=40 multi-task). The
   layer_bk -8 aggregate is a negative-ROI experiment; re-run BK at
   n=3 only after fixtures validated against another variant. Save ~$2.

3. *Move K11+K17 into a "premium pool" with $1.50/round cap each.*
   K11 alone consumed 35% of R24 for 15% of scoring volume; K17 is
   promising but 4.4x K1's per-cell cost. Bounded pool resolves the
   credit-cliff pattern the R20-R23 supplement flagged.

4. *Diagnose K7 lint runaway BEFORE re-running at n>=5.* B1 cells
   produced primary=28 (K1-tier) gated to score=-108 by bracket lint.
   Either floor lint penalty at score=0 or fix K7 output format
   upstream; re-probing at production n without diagnosis wastes $0.77.

5. *Schedule BK1-BK5 into layer-1 with 3+ FOSS variants (K1, K8, K17).*
   R24's layer_bk fired only on K2/K5; long-horizon signal is still
   under-sampled. Budget ~$0.50 for 3 variants x 5 BK x 1 cell.

* Summary

*Summary.* R18 v2 ran 140 cells / $2.770 across 10 variants × 8 tasks
× ≤8 dial labels. *50 ok cells, 90 worktree-collision errors* (64%
error rate; affects multi-task variants asymmetrically — K8 lost 14/15
layer-2 attempts, K1 lost 14, K11/K17 lost 9 each). Most cost-per-quality
numbers below are anchored on either layer-1 (B1, n=1) or the n=7
paired layer-2 sweep where K1+K5+K6+K7 actually completed.

*Headline.* On the apples-to-apples *layer-2 paired sweep*, the FOSS
frontier collapses to *K1-qwen30 + K6-llama70b* with K7-qwen72b sitting
just off-frontier (3.4× more expensive for ~80% of K1's score).
*K11-qwen3coder is dominated again — 38× the price of K1 for equal
score on its only B1 cell.* K2-kimi-k2.6 is silent (broker-format bug,
score=0 on all 15 cells).

*Expanded.* The big R18-vs-R17 frontier shift: K6-llama70b moved
*onto* the frontier (was n=1 / score=4 in R17, now competitive at
n=14 / score=66 across 8 tasks). K8-deepseekV3 fell *off* the
frontier purely because worktree errors blocked its layer-2 cells —
the one B1 cell it ran lands at $0.00095/qpt (still good). R19 must
fix the worktree-collision retry before any K8/K11 conclusion is real.

** Headline surprises

1. *K9-mixtral 3× projected* — $0.1147 vs R17's $0.041. Re-priced or
   longer wall. *Drop K9 from R19 default roster unless re-validated.*
2. *K11 still bizarrely expensive* — $0.3835 single B1 cell, 78% over
   R17. Coder-style verbose chain-of-tool-calls is the anti-pattern.
3. *K6 doubled per-cell cost* but score jumped n=1/4 → n=14/66.
   Net $/qpt improved — cost rise is layer-2 prose burning more tokens.
4. *K15-kimi-thinking got CHEAPER* (-32%). Caching helping on recurring
   tail. R19 should *measure caching realized %* per variant.
5. *K5 now $1.77/round* — first round where Claude pilot spend dwarfs
   FOSS spend ($0.998 FOSS vs $1.77 Claude). 23× headroom holds
   per-quality (K1 26× cheaper) but absolute is no longer rounding to 0.
6. *Total $2.77* vs $80-200 design projection — 30-70× under-spent
   because worktree errors killed 90/140 cells before they could spend.
   "Real" R18 cost (had errors not blocked cells) projects ~$8-12.

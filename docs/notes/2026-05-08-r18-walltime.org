#+TITLE: R18 v2 — walltime + parallelism efficiency analysis
#+FILETAGS: :wiki:multi-agent-org-llm:bench:duration:r18:
#+OPTIONS: toc:1 num:nil

* Summary

R18 v2 ran 140 cells in *28.1 min wall* (1687s) at 16-way parallel,
producing 50 successful cells + 90 worktree-collision errors. Per-cell
wall distribution is nearly identical to R17 (median 29s, p90 183s,
p99 ~600s). *Effective parallelism was only 2.57× (16% of the 16-way
ceiling)* — but the cause is structural: each layer is max-cell-bound,
not worker-bound. Layer-barrier overhead is essentially zero (~1.6s),
and layer1 startup overhead is ~60s. The wall is dictated by the
single longest cell per layer (K5-claude on B5: 648s; K7-qwen72b
ablations: 577s; K15-kimi: 399s), so adding more workers cannot help.

* 1. Total wall vs R17

| Round  | Cells | Workers | Successful | Wall (min) | Wall (s) | Cells/min (success) |
|--------+-------+---------+------------+------------+----------+---------------------|
| R17    |    59 |       8 |         59 |       20.0 |     1200 |                 3.0 |
| R18 v2 |   140 |      16 |         50 |       28.1 |     1687 |                1.78 |

R18 v2 *appears* slower than R17 in cells/min, but:
- 90/140 cells were worktree-creation errors that took ~1s each (no
  real work) — they were the layer1 stress-test footprint.
- The 50 *successful* cells include the longest-tail variants ever
  benched: K5-claude on B5 = 648s, K7-qwen72b dial-ablation = 577s.
- True comparison: 50 R18 success cells in 28.1 min ≈ R17's 59 cells
  in 20 min when normalized by sum-of-walls (R17 ~3h, R18 ~1.2h).

* 4. Parallel efficiency

| Layer  | Cells | Wall (s) | Sum-of-walls | Max cell | Theor. floor | Overhead | Eff. parallelism |
|--------+-------+----------+--------------+----------+--------------+----------+------------------|
| layer1 |   100 |      459 |       1337.0 |    398.8 |        398.8 |    +60.2 |             2.91 |
| layer2 |    28 |      650 |       1331.0 |    648.7 |        648.7 |     +1.3 |             2.05 |
| layer3 |    12 |      579 |       1664.7 |    577.4 |        577.4 |     +1.6 |             2.88 |

Theoretical floor = max(longest cell, sum_of_walls / 16). When floor
≈ wall, the layer is *max-cell-bound* (single slow cell dictates wall;
adding workers cannot help).

- Overall: sum-of-walls 4333s / wallclock 1688s = *2.57× effective
  parallelism* = 16.0% of theoretical 16-way ceiling.
- This is *not a worker-utilization failure*. With 50 success cells
  and a max cell of 648s, the wall floor is 648s per layer + transitions.
- Layer1 + layer3 efficiency is bounded by single hot cells; layer2's
  efficiency looks low (2.05) because most layer2 cells are very fast
  (<30s) and one K5-claude cell (648s) holds the layer open.

* 5. Layer-barrier overhead

| Transition       | Time (HH:MM:SS) | Δ from prev (s) |
|------------------+-----------------+-----------------|
| LAYER 1 start    |        02:04:48 |               — |
| LAYER 2 start    |        02:12:27 |             459 |
| LAYER 3 start    |        02:23:17 |             650 |
| R18 COMPLETE     |        02:32:56 |             579 |

- Layer1: 90 cells finished by 02:06:03 (75s in), but K15-kimi held
  the layer until 02:12:27 (final cell 398.8s). Pure barrier overhead
  ≈ 60s (worktree-creation startup for 100 cells, mostly errors).
- Layer2: 125 of 128 cells finished by 02:14:03 (cell 125 done at
  +97s into layer2). *Cells 126-128 took an additional 553s* — all
  K5-claude-solo cells, the K5/B5 cell ran 648s. Idle workers
  (16 - 1 = 15) sat idle for ~9 minutes.
- Layer3: 12 cells finished in 579s, last cell 577.4s. Barrier
  overhead 1.6s — perfect. K7-qwen72b ablations ran the full window.

*Idle-worker-time during layer2 long-tail*: ~15 workers × 553s ≈
8295 worker-seconds wasted. If pipelined, layer3 could have started
at +97s into layer2 instead of waiting → 553s saved.

* 7. R19 wall projections

R19 adds: LoRA-distilled K20 model + Qdrant vault RAG + 5 long-horizon
fixtures (BK1-BK5).

| Workload          | Cells | p50 wall | p90 wall | Sum (s) | 16-way wall (s) |
|-------------------+-------+----------+----------+---------+-----------------|
| R18 baseline      |    50 |       29 |      183 |    4333 |            1688 |
| + LoRA K20 var    |   +50 |   ~5-15? |    ~30?  |    +500 |             ~30 |
| + RAG-enabled     |   +20 |    +5-10 |     +20  |    +200 |             ~20 |
| + BK1-BK5 long    |    +5 |     ~600 |    ~1500 |   +5000 |            ~1500 |
| Total R19 (proj.) |  ~125 |       30 |      300 |  ~10000 |       ~1700-3500 |

Notes on each addition:
- *LoRA K20*: Modal-served sub-second TTFT, 7B model. Per-cell wall
  ~5-15s estimated (faster than K6-llama70b's 16s median because
  smaller + Modal). Adds ~50 cells, total ~500s sum-wall, fits in
  single max-cell layer = ~30s wall.
- *RAG-enabled*: Each retrieval adds ~5-10s (Qdrant RTT + embed). If
  layered onto existing 50 cells: +5-10s × 50 = ~250-500s sum-wall,
  ~10-20s wall added (parallel-absorbed).
- *Long-horizon BK1-BK5*: 5 cells at p50 ~600s, p90 ~1500s. *These
  will dominate wall*. Even if perfectly parallel, max cell ~1500s
  becomes the new floor.

R19 projected total wall:

| Scenario               | Wall    | Notes                                  |
|------------------------+---------+----------------------------------------|
| Sequential layers      |  ~50min | Layer-by-layer; BK long-tail dominates |
| Pipelined layers       |  ~30min | If BK starts in parallel with R18-bench |
| With layer-pipelining  |  ~25min | Pre-launch BK as background pool       |
| With hedged retry      |  ~20min | Spawn BK on 2 providers; first wins    |

Recommended R19 strategy:
1. *Pipeline layer2 → layer3*: launch layer3 cells as soon as 80% of
   layer2 cells are done (skip the long-tail wait). Saves ~9 min
   based on R18 v2's K5-claude tail.
2. *Hard wall cap = 600s*: kill any cell >10 min, mark as
   "wall-capped" and let scoring handle it. Prevents single-cell
   serialization.
3. *Hedged retry on cells >300s* (p90): spawn duplicate on fallback
   provider; first-to-finish wins. Cuts long-tail by ~30%.
4. *BK1-BK5 in their own pool*: long-horizon cells run as background
   pool from t=0; don't block layered bench.

Without these mitigations, R19 wall projects to ~50 min (long-tail
serialization). With pipelining + hedging, ~20-25 min for ~125 cells.

#+TITLE: R25 — walltime + WALL_CAP enforcement analysis (PARALLELISM=32, cap=400s)
#+FILETAGS: :wiki:multi-agent-org-llm:bench:duration:r25:
#+OPTIONS: toc:1 num:nil

* Summary

R25 ran *189 cells in 39m23s wall* (2363s) at *32-way parallel* —
*15 min faster than R24 with 28% more cells* (R24: 147 / 54.3 min).
The =WALL_CAP_PER_CELL_S=400= enforcement (R25 P9) *fired 16 times,
100% on K2-kimi-k2.6 / B1* — but the =ThreadPoolExecutor= shim around
=run_one_cell= waits at context-exit for the inner thread, so the cap
*records the kill* without freeing the outer worker. K2 killed-cell
=wall_seconds= ranges 455-1697s, far past the 400s ceiling. Layer 1
remained bound at 1723s (28.7 min) — same shape as R24. The cap
ships a label, not the latency floor it advertises. R25 still beat
R24 on wall because K7-qwen72b dropped (Sp4) and Layers 2/3/BK/K11
all ran near theoretical-ceiling parallelism. Fixing the cap is R26's
single biggest lever — real outer-thread kill drops L1 to ~650s.

* 3. WALL_CAP enforcement — fired but did not free workers

*Summary.* The cap raised =TimeoutError= on 16 K2/B1 cells, wrote
=error: wall_cap_killed:400s= into result records, and printed the
WALL_CAP_KILLED log line at the 400s mark. But the surrounding =with
ThreadPoolExecutor= context manager calls =shutdown(wait=True)= on
exit, blocking the outer worker until the inner thread finishes.

*Expanded.* From =scripts/_round25_dials.py:1681=:

#+begin_src python
with _TPE(max_workers=1) as _inner:
    _fut = _inner.submit(run_one_cell, ...)
    cell = _fut.result(timeout=_wall_cap)   # raises after 400s
# at __exit__, shutdown(wait=True) blocks until _fut completes
#+end_src

Evidence: WALL_CAP_KILLED log lines fire spread across the full
1723s L1 wall (10:47:37 first, 11:07:22 last — 1185s spread). If the
cap freed workers, the 16 kills would cluster within ~30s near the
400s mark. They trail the layer because each outer worker is pinned
waiting for its zombie inner thread. FIFO@32 with K2 walls clamped
to 400s = *650s* L1 wall instead of 1723s. Cap under-delivered ~1073s.

* 4. Why is K2 100% of WALL_CAP kills?

*Summary.* K2 routes via OpenRouter → Moonshot/Parasail.
=MODAL_KIMI_ENABLED= was *not set*, so the self-hosted vLLM endpoint
was bypassed. Both brokers exhibit multi-second TTFT and 60-180s
prefill stalls on long prefixes (B1 = 5691 chars). With =max_tokens=4096=
and =n=50= sample-up, K2 was the only variant reliably exceeding 400s.

*Expanded.* Per-variant aggregates:

| Variant         | n  | sum (s) | max (s) | med (s) | mean (s) | killed |
|-----------------+----+---------+---------+---------+----------+--------|
| K2-kimi-k2.6    | 50 |  19,598 |  1697.7 |   272.5 |    392.0 | *16/50*|
| K8-deepseekV3   | 29 |   3,213 |   241.0 |   107.3 |    110.8 |    0   |
| K17-glm46       | 10 |   2,185 |   253.0 |   228.9 |    218.5 |    0   |
| K1-qwen30       | 65 |   2,169 |   106.0 |    23.2 |     33.4 |    0   |
| K5-claude-solo  | 14 |   1,126 |   380.6 |    41.0 |     80.4 |    0   |
| K11-qwen3coder  | 18 |   1,111 |   139.0 |    53.4 |     61.7 |    0   |
| K33/K34/K35     |  3 |     137 |   108.0 |    14.0 |     45.7 |    0   |

K2 owns *66% of round walls* (19,598/29,538) on *26% of cells*
(50/189). K2 is bimodal: 19 cells <100s, 16 cells >800s. Hypotheses:

1. *Broker prefill stall*. Moonshot exhibits 60-180s TTFT under load.
   =["Moonshot", "Parasail"]= preference means Moonshot is tried first;
   on Moonshot timeout, Parasail inherits with its own prefill latency.
2. *max_tokens=4096 isn't enough*. K2 still hits length-limit; 4096
   tokens at 30-40 tok/s = 100-140s output time alone. With prefill,
   ~200-300s is the happy path.
3. *32-way parallelism amplifies broker queue*. ~8-12 K2 cells in
   flight against one Moonshot endpoint compounds queue time. R24 saw
   the same K2/B1 outlier (1612s) at 32-way.
4. *No retry budget / no HTTP timeout*. Hanging broker → cell waits
   indefinitely until WALL_CAP fires.

* 6. Why didn't Layer 3 / Layer-BK / comp fully run?

*Summary.* All scheduled layers ran to completion in 1m45s after L1.
Shrunken cell counts are by-design dedup: L1's =top3_foss= resolved to
*=['K1-qwen30', 'K1-qwen30', 'K1-qwen30']=* — same variant 3× —
because K1 won B1 quality and K2 took 16 zero-score wall-cap kills.
Downstream layers saw 1-2 unique FOSS variants.

12) because dedup; results still valid for K1 but lack K2/K11
counterpoint. Layer-BK ran 5 tasks × (K1 + K5) = 10 cells in 82s —
*faster than R24's 157s* at same cell count.

* 7. R26 advisory — fix the cap, swap K2 broker, hold parallelism

*Summary.* Three R26 actions ranked by Layer-1-wall savings:

| Rank | Action                          | Wall save     | Confidence | Cost     |
|------+---------------------------------+---------------+------------+----------|
| 1    | Fix WALL_CAP outer-kill         | -1073s on L1  | high       | 1 patch  |
| 2    | Self-host K2 via Modal          | -800-1500s    | high       | env flag |
| 3    | Reduce K2 n=50 → n=20 in L1     | -300s         | medium     | 1 const  |
| 4    | Hold PARALLELISM=32             | 0s            | high       | -        |
| 5    | Drop WALL_CAP to 300s           | +risk on K2   | low        | 1 const  |

*Expanded.*

1. *Fix WALL_CAP outer-kill* (PM3-bis). Replace =ThreadPoolExecutor=
   shim with =multiprocessing.Process= (terminate-able) OR a
   =threading.Event= that =run_one_cell= polls to abort in-flight HTTP.
   Current shim is /worse than no cap/ — eats CPU on zombie threads and
   pretends to enforce. Acceptance: L1 lands ≤650s on rerun.
2. *K2 broker swap to Modal-Kimi*. =MODAL_KIMI_ENABLED=1= routes K2 to
   =org-llm-kimi-k26-vllm-serve.modal.run=. R18 quoted "sub-second TTFT
   vs Parasail's 2-200s tail." Even at half-quote, K2 median 272s →
   ~80s and outliers stop. Verify endpoint health pre-R26; cold-start
   adds 30-60s to first cell.
3. *K2 n=50 → n=20 in L1*. With Modal-Kimi the broker variance
   collapses; n=20 is enough for p10/p90 CIs. Saves ~300s.
4. *Hold PARALLELISM=32*. L2/L3/BK/L-K11 are at theoretical ceiling.
   With cap+Modal, L1 FIFO@32 ≈ 400s; @64 ≈ 250s — not worth 32 more fds.
5. *Reject* cap=300s. K2's honest median (excluding kills) is 272s; a
   300s cap kills normal K2 cells. Hold 400s; fix enforcement.

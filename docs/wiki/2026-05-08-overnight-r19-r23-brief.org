02:25 and 05:01 EDT. R19 produced the strongest FOSS-vs-Claude
result on record — K1-qwen30 at $0.00047 per quality unit, *51×
cheaper* than K5-claude-solo at $0.02423/u. R20 confirmed K2-kimi

K5-claude-solo scored — they're effectively no-data rounds for
FOSS analysis.

*Expanded.* The R19 wiring patch (BK1-BK5 long-horizon tasks +
provider.ignore deny-lists + quality_lint N5 penalty) all landed
correctly and runs were healthy. The round-chain autopilot's
sed-based "fork + delta" worked flawlessly through R23 — every
harness committed, every preflight syntax-checked. The hard stop
was OpenRouter's credit cap ($30 used = $30 limit at 04:39).
After credits are topped up, R21-R23 can be re-run with their
forked harnesses already on disk.

** Worktree fix (fc59d1c)

The =safe_worktree_add= retry-on-collision change shipped before
R19 ran. Comparing R18 v3 (140 cells with ~64% worktree-wiped) to
R19 (120 cells with 0 worktree errors visible in stdout) suggests
the fix is working. R19's cell counts now reflect real attempts,
not error-padded counters.

** R19 wiring patch (7ca05fc)

- BK1-BK5 imported + appended to TASKS

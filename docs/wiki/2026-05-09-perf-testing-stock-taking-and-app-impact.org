** 5c — Pre-round "what will this round teach us?" template

Adopt for R29+: every round design opens with a 4-row table:

| Question                                          | Round's answer plan                          | Confidence |
|---------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------+------------|
| (1) What's the headline question?                 | (test type + cells + bar)                     | (40-90%)   |
| (2) What's the smallest-reading-of-symptom probe? | (1-cell or mini-A/B variant)                  | (40-90%)   |
| (3) What's the app-surface impact if YES?         | (verb / surface / phase)                      | (BENCH/APP)|
| (4) What's the app-surface impact if NO?          | (next-round design implication)               | (n/a)      |

Round retro then grades: did the round actually answer (1)? Did the
smallest probe (2) gate the larger work? Did the app-surface (3/4)
land or did the team forget about it?

* 7 — Recommendations for R29-R33 arc

*Summary.* Five recommendations for the next 5 rounds. Top-line: keep
running the bench /but/ shift weight off "more rounds" and onto
"productize what we already learned." A bench-step-away point lives
between R31 and R32: when the next round's marginal learning < the
already-shippable backlog's marginal value, ship.

*Expanded.* Per [Lean assess + iterate] the next 5 rounds should look
smaller, not bigger. R29 is the right size already (~200 cells, $8-15);
R30 should mirror; R31 the K20 v2 retrain validation; R32 a
productization sprint with one verification probe; R33 a pure ship-
sprint OR (more likely) the bench winds down to "as-needed" mode while
the app integrates.

** 7b — When to step away from the bench

The arc earns the right to step away when /at least 3/ of these hold:

| Signal                                                                | R28 status      |
|-----------------------------------------------------------------------+-----------------|
| (a) Top 12 productization picks all have phase rows in roadmap.org    | NOT YET (5 propose-rows pending) |
| (b) FOSS crosses Claude on quality with verifier-honest evidence       | NEAR (R29-16 hardens)            |
| (c) Watchdog/preflight/cancel primitives ship as user-facing surfaces | NOT YET (this doc's recommendation) |
| (d) Per-task slicing is the default lens in user-facing leaderboard   | NOT YET (R28-U2 not landed)      |
| (e) Bench infra is in CI / nightly cadence, not eng-driven sweeps     | NOT YET                          |
| (f) Three rounds in a row produce only confirmation, not new findings  | NO (R28 produced K6 + watchdog)  |

R32-R33 is the realistic step-away window. If R29 + R30 land their
phase-row commitments and R31 is K6 quality survives the preservation
gate, R32 becomes a legitimate "ship more, bench less" round.

** 7c — One-line decision rule for round-vs-ship

If the next planned round's top hypothesis is "we already know this is
true; we're confirming for app-confidence" — *ship the app surface
first, then run a 50-cell confirmation probe* (T-7 type per
=perf-testing-roadmap §test types catalog=), not a 200-cell full round.

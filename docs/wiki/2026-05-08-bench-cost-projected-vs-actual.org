#+TITLE: Bench cost — projected vs actual across rounds (R15-R18)
#+FILETAGS: :wiki:multi-agent-org-llm:bench:cost:
#+OPTIONS: toc:1 num:nil
#+CATEGORY: bench

#+STARTUP: overview content

* Summary

*Summary.* Both R15 and R16 spent ~13-50× LESS than projected. Cost
ceiling has not been the binding constraint across 3 rounds; wall
time and observability are. R17/R18 budgets can be much more
ambitious than the $100/$300 design-doc tables suggest — actual
spend will likely land $5-15 (R17) and $15-50 (R18).

*Expanded.* Cost projections in design docs were based on per-cell
ceilings (e.g. $5/cell × 54 cells = $270 worst case) but actual
per-cell spend is dominated by very-fast FOSS cells in the $0.001-
0.05 range. Outlier cells (K7 thrash, Claude-on-B11) push p90 wall
to 191s but median stays at 31s. Designing the next round, treat
the cost cap as a sanity floor, not a constraint.

* Round-by-round projected vs actual

| Round | Projected               | Actual          | Ratio   |
|-------+-------------------------+-----------------+---------|
| R15   | $15-25 (lean), $25 cap | $1.62           | ~13× cheaper |
| R16   | $40-60 (lean), $80-100 (full), $100 cap | $2.07 | ~25-50× cheaper |
| R17 (proj.) | $50-100 / $100 cap | (running) | est ~$5-15 |
| R18 (proj.) | $80-200 / $300 cap | TBD | est ~$15-50 |

* Why projections keep overshooting

** Reason 1 — most FOSS cells finish in <$0.01

R16 cost-distribution ((54 cells, $2.07 total):

| Percentile | Cell cost |
|------------+-----------|
| min        | $0.0005   |
| median     | $0.012    |
| p90        | $0.092    |
| max        | $0.296    |

Median $0.012, not the projected $0.75-1.85. The 8000-token
max_iter ceiling almost never gets hit — most cells finish in
1-3 iterations.

** Reason 2 — "dead" variants fail cheap

R16 had 5 dead variants (K3, K4, K10, K12, K14) that silent_noop'd
or HTTP-failed in 1 iteration each, costing ~$0 collectively. Pre-
flight rejection would save the wall time but the cost was
already negligible.

** Reason 3 — Claude isn't the cost dominator I projected

R16 K5-claude-solo: 8 cells × ~$0.13 = ~$1.05. Half the total
spend, but absolute cost is tiny because Claude's per-iter is
constrained by the iteration count + max_tokens.

** Reason 4 — adaptive abort + per-task tool gating cap thrash cost

R16's K7-qwen72b max cell: $0.30 wall = 1169s. Without R17's
adaptive abort + per-task tool gating, this could have been
$1-2/cell on the longest thrash iterations. The guards are working.

** Reason 5 — K-tier new variants have $0 floors

K10/K11/K12/K14 each cost $0.001-0.082 in their single R16 cells.
The expected scaling-law-driven premium (K12 llama-405b should
cost more than K6 llama-70b) didn't materialize because most
cells failed before consuming context.

* Implications for R17 / R18 design

** Don't budget by ceiling

Treat the cost cap as a sanity floor (catches runaway loops),
not a binding constraint on round design. R18's overnight scope
can include all Tracks D-P without budget anxiety.

** Wall time + signal quality are the real constraints

| Constraint | R16 evidence |
|------------+--------------|
| Wall time  | 1h 32min sequential; need 8-way parallelism (R17) for any larger sweep |
| Signal/n   | n=1 leaves variance unmeasurable (per variance agent); n≥3 needed for confident calls |
| Quality scoring | Mech score misranks; quality judge needed (per quality judge agent) |
| Observability | Cell-level diff hygiene + per-iter logs needed (R17 has these) |

** Actual cost trajectory

R15 → R16 → R17 (proj.) → R18 (proj.):
- $0.033/cell → $0.038/cell → $0.06/cell → $0.10/cell

The $0.10/cell R18 estimate is high-side because:
- More premium variants (K11 480B, K12 405B, K13 1.6T, K15 thinking)
- More iter ceiling (16 → 32 for thinking models)
- More cells per task (n≥3 minimum)
- Multi-LLM composition (R18 Track N: ~3× cells in CN1-CN7)

Even at $0.10/cell × 600 cells = $60. Below the $300 cap by 5×.

* The lever this opens

R18 can deploy infrastructure that compounds long-term:

| Track | What | Cost |
|-------+------+------|
| LoRA training (R18 Track G) | Modal A100, 6-10h, $13-22 once-per-version | one-shot |
| RAG index (R18 Track D)     | One-time index build ~$0.50; per-query 10ms | amortized to ~$0 |
| Modal-Kimi self-host        | $26/month idle volume + $4/h running | flat |
| Observer LLM (R18 Track F)  | qwen30 cheap; +$0.001 per cell-iteration | linear |
| Cross-run learning (R18+)   | Re-feed prior best results as exemplars | $0 |

Total infrastructure investment for R18 ≤ ~$50; round itself ≤
~$80; combined ≤ $150 — still well under the $300 cap.

* Caveat

If R18 explores expensive territory we haven't priced (e.g.
self-hosting a 405B+ model on multi-GPU shard for a long run),
costs could climb. The flagship K13-deepseek-V4-pro (1.6T MoE
on OpenRouter) is the single highest-cost-risk variant in the
roster — needs n≥3 cells before promoting widely.

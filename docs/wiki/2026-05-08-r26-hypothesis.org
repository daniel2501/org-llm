* Summary

*Summary.* This is a pre-commit hypothesis for R26: predicted
variant scores, $/qpt, Pareto positions, strategy outcomes, infra
behavior, and total spend — each with an explicit confidence
percentage and a falsification criterion. After R26 lands, a
post-round measurement pass scores the predictions against
observed reality. The /prediction-accuracy score/ is meta-bench
signal on whether our model of the system is right.

*Expanded.* The bench has produced six rounds of converging
evidence (R15, R16, R17, R18, R19/R20, R24, R25). With R25's three
bug fixes applied (top-3 selector dedupe, =count_bracket_errors=
per-line walk, real WALL_CAP kill plus K2 =max_tokens= wired) we
should be measuring the system-as-designed for the first time.
Predictions below codify the priors that fall out of the synthesis
docs and case studies — explicit so we can be wrong out loud, not
hedge in retrospect.

* Strategy predictions (S1-S4)

** S1 hedged escalation (K8 → K11)

*Summary.* 8-15 K8 cells fall below threshold (=primary < 12=) and
escalate to K11; total escalation cost $0.40-$0.90. Confidence:
55%.

*Expanded.* =HEDGED_QUALITY_THRESHOLD = 12= and K8 generalization
is unresolved — non-B1 K8 cells likely produce 0/abstain a few
times, triggering escalation. Falsified if 0 escalations OR >25
escalations.

** S2 best-of-N (5× K8 on B5/B11/B20/B45)

*Summary.* Best-of-5 K8 on B5/B11/B20 lands within 15% of single
K2's mean for those tasks but at <50% K2 cost. Confidence: 50%.

*Expanded.* =BEST_OF_N_VARIANT = "K8-deepseekV3"=,
=BEST_OF_N_SAMPLES = 5=, =BEST_OF_N_TASKS = {B5,B11,B20,B45}=. K8
on prose is the explicit test. Falsified if best-of-5 K8 mean
>20% below single K2 OR cost exceeds K2 single-shot.

** S3 critique-revise (K1 → K2 → K1 on B11 + B5)

*Summary.* Final revised K1 score on B11+B5 lifts ≥3 points over
raw K1. Confidence: 55%.

*Expanded.* The orphan-since-R18 strategy now wired. K1 has
known bimodal pattern on B11 (50,50,0,0,0,12.5 in R17). Critique
pass should pull bottom modes up. Falsified if mean lift < 1.5
points OR critique cost exceeds direct K2 on same task.

** S4 specialty routing (=TASK_FAMILY_VARIANTS=)

*Summary.* Filter drops 30-50% of L2 cell-specs (variants not
allowed for that task family). Confidence: 80%.

*Expanded.* =TASK_FAMILY_VARIANTS= maps wiki_edit→{K1,K8},
code_edit→{K8,K11}, elisp→{K2}, dec_draft→{K11,K2,K5},
small_atomic→{K1,K8}. With ~5 advancing variants × 5 tasks ≈ 25
specs pre-S4, expect ~12-18 post-S4. Falsified if filter drops
0 cells OR drops >70% (over-restrictive).

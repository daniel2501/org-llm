** Kimi-reasoning warmup-probe drops K2 from round
:PROPERTIES:
:OBSERVED: 17:33:37 EST 2026-05-09
:END:
*Summary.* K2-kimi-k2.6 dropped at warmup (=no PING match (got: '')=)
because Kimi-K2.6 emits its short reply via =message.reasoning=
(chain-of-thought enabled by default), leaving =message.content= null.
Warmup probe checked content only.
*Why surprising.* =specialist.py:_DISABLE_REASONING_MODELS= already
includes =moonshotai/kimi-k2.6= for in-band cell calls. Warmup just
didn't use the same flag. Symmetric-config drift between two functions
in the same module.
*Fix.* =_round28_dials.py:203-= now has =_WARMUP_REASONING_DISABLE=
set; payload gets =reasoning: {enabled: false}= for those slugs.
Live-verified: K2 warmup returns =ok=True via Parasail=.
*R29 input.* Audit other =DISABLE_REASONING= sites to ensure all
Kimi-touching paths drop reasoning consistently. (Currently: warmup +
in-band — other paths?)

** CAT 3 K20 endpoint resume budget at watchdog edge
:PROPERTIES:
:OBSERVED: 17:24-17:33 EST 2026-05-09
:END:
*Summary.* CAT 3 K20 endpoint resume (Together-managed) took 8m6s.
Watchdog budget set at 480s (8m). Stall-detect didn't fire (heartbeat
for CAT 4 came right before watchdog poll noticed CAT 3 was over).
*Why surprising.* The 8min budget was sized for "typical" Together
cold-start; the variance pushed past it. Saved by 15s poll cadence,
not by margin.
*Fix.* Bump =STAGE_BUDGET[PREFLIGHT_CAT_3_K20_RESUME]=480 → 600=
(10 min). 25% margin over observed worst-case.
*R29 input.* Add stage-budget tuning to R29 retro item.

** B1 K20 cells wall-capping at >400s — long-context not fully clamped?
:PROPERTIES:
:OBSERVED: 17:46:14 EST 2026-05-09 (and continuing)
:END:
*Summary.* Two K20-foss-distill cells on B1 hit WALL_CAP_KILLED at
>400s. R28-1 cancel/sentinel fired correctly. But /why/ are K20 cells
on B1 taking >400s? B1 is wiki_edit with a long primer; max_tokens
clamp (P0-4) reduces output ceiling but doesn't change input length.
*Why surprising.* P0-4 was framed as fixing HTTP 400 ctx-overflow —
not fixing /latency/. Long-context inference on the LoRA may
genuinely take >400s if the prompt is large enough.
*Fix candidates.*
1. Per-task =WALL_CAP_BY_CLASS= bump for K20 (already exists for
    other variants).
2. Aggressive context truncation for K20 wiki_edit cells.
3. Longer wall-cap for K20 specifically.
*R29 input.* Inspect R28 K20-on-B1 wall-cap cells; were they HTTP
400s (P0-4 clamp didn't catch them) or genuinely-slow inference (need
longer cap or smaller context)?

* Strategic guidance (R28 changes how org-llm should route + ship)

*Summary.* Six directional changes R28 supports, ranked by strategic
weight. Companion to =bench-to-app-mapping.org §R28-derived additions=
(the *what's-shippable* lens); this section is the *what-should-change-
in-our-thinking* lens.

*Expanded.* Each guidance item: change + R28 evidence + caveats + app
impact. These are *directional* — they may need expanded validation
(e.g. G2 K5 deprecation warrants its own evidence-gathering round).

** G1 — Routing defaults: K8 + K6 are the new default backbone

K8-deepseekV3 = workhorse default (44% cell share, primary mean 8.15,
*best $/u FOSS at $0.00071*). K6-Llama-3.3-70b = quality-tier handoff
(primary mean 25.14 — wide-margin leader). K1-qwen30 = budget-tier
draft. App impact: =org_llm/specialist.py= default + =cli.py= =--model=
default → K8. Quality-tier verbs (=write-dec=, =draft-essay=,
=refine-prose=) → K6. DEC-007 update with R28 evidence.

** G2 — Claude (K5) sentinel deprecation candidate

K6 beat K5-Claude 2.01× on primary mean (25.14 vs 12.52) across 57
same-round cells. K5 was the only variant with fab (4/21). K5 cost
18× K8. Three independent gates support deprecation. *Caveats:* R28
is a small focused suite; K5 may shine on tasks R28 didn't probe
(long-form synthesis, multi-step planning). *App impact:* remove K5
from default cloud catalog; opt-in only. *R29 verification:* expanded
K5-vs-K6 head-to-head (B5/B11/B25/B26/B45 + 5 BK at N=10) before
acting on app-side change.

** G3 — K2 (Kimi) demote to opt-in

K2 mini-A/B: +0.25 mean over K1 at 24× cost. Marginal lift; not worth
default routing. *App impact:* K2 stays in cloud catalog as opt-in for
long-context (>16k) or specific Kimi behavior; not in default agent
roster.

** G4 — K20 LoRA strategy: invest in v2 retrain

K20 v1 went 0/10 → 10/10 across one round of harness fixes. The LoRA
itself is solid; access path was the bottleneck. *Now we have a clean
baseline.* v2 corpus = R26-R28 wins where K20 was distilled-for. Goal:
push K20 mean from 7.0 toward K6's 25.14. *App impact:* unlocks
=/train-lora= verb as user-facing feature — distill from your vault's
edit history into a personal LoRA, served on $1.39/hr RunPod.

** G5 — Per-task slicing must be the primary scoring lens

K1's R28 primary mean (9.26) appears below R27's (16.7) — but the
delta is dominated by task-shape distribution, not actual K1
regression. R28 ran more BK long-horizon cells; K1's L1 quality may
be unchanged. *App impact:* =org-llm-status-buffer= default view =
per-task slicing, not aggregate. Aggregate is fine for compute-budget
planning; useless for quality-regression detection. R28 quality-judge
scour must produce K1-by-task to confirm.

** G6 — Watchdog + heartbeat are the default for any long-running op

Pre-watchdog: 9-min silent stall before any signal surfaced.
Post-watchdog: per-stage heartbeats + budgets + audible alert. *App
impact:* =org_llm/watchdog.py= primitive (Python class wrapping the
bash pattern). Verbs that adopt: =sync=, =train=, =index=, =review=,
=captain=, =agor=. UI: modeline cookie =[stage 12s/30s]= shows
non-progress before it's a wall.

** Kimi-reasoning warmup-probe drops K2 from round
:PROPERTIES:

*Summary.* K2-kimi-k2.6 dropped at warmup (=no PING match (got: '')=)
because Kimi-K2.6 emits its short reply via =message.reasoning=
(chain-of-thought enabled by default), leaving =message.content= null.
Warmup probe checked content only.
*Why surprising.* =specialist.py:_DISABLE_REASONING_MODELS= already
includes =moonshotai/kimi-k2.6= for in-band cell calls. Warmup just
didn't use the same flag. Symmetric-config drift between two functions
in the same module.
*Fix.* =_round28_dials.py:203-= now has =_WARMUP_REASONING_DISABLE=
set; payload gets =reasoning: {enabled: false}= for those slugs.
Live-verified: K2 warmup returns =ok=True via Parasail=.

Kimi-touching paths drop reasoning consistently. (Currently: warmup +
in-band — other paths?)

** CAT 3 K20 endpoint resume budget at watchdog edge
:PROPERTIES:
:OBSERVED: 17:24-17:33 EST 2026-05-09
:END:
*Summary.* CAT 3 K20 endpoint resume (Together-managed) took 8m6s.
Watchdog budget set at 480s (8m). Stall-detect didn't fire (heartbeat
for CAT 4 came right before watchdog poll noticed CAT 3 was over).
*Why surprising.* The 8min budget was sized for "typical" Together
cold-start; the variance pushed past it. Saved by 15s poll cadence,
not by margin.
*Fix.* Bump =STAGE_BUDGET[PREFLIGHT_CAT_3_K20_RESUME]=480 → 600=
(10 min). 25% margin over observed worst-case.
*R29 input.* Add stage-budget tuning to R29 retro item.

** B1 K20 cells wall-capping at >400s — long-context not fully clamped?
:PROPERTIES:
:OBSERVED: 17:46:14 EST 2026-05-09 (and continuing)
:END:
*Summary.* Two K20-foss-distill cells on B1 hit WALL_CAP_KILLED at
>400s. R28-1 cancel/sentinel fired correctly. But /why/ are K20 cells
on B1 taking >400s? B1 is wiki_edit with a long primer; max_tokens
clamp (P0-4) reduces output ceiling but doesn't change input length.
*Why surprising.* P0-4 was framed as fixing HTTP 400 ctx-overflow —
not fixing /latency/. Long-context inference on the LoRA may
genuinely take >400s if the prompt is large enough.
*Resolved by post-scour analysis (2026-05-09 PM EST).* Per
=r28-quality-judge.org §6= + =r28-walltime.org §6=: actually *4*
K20-on-B1 cells JSON-killed (synthesis under-counted). Cause is
*16-iteration tool-loop ~25s/iter* on long-context wiki edit, NOT
HTTP 400 ctx-overflow. P0-4 clamp WORKED (H7 PASS); the wall-cap
fires because tool-loop iteration is slow on the LoRA, not because
context overflows. Mitigation per quality-judge: restrict K20 to
*BK family only* (not B1 wiki_edit) — K20 v1 was distilled on small-
atomic + BK shapes; B1's verbose-prefix primer is out of distribution.
*R29 input.* R29-15 — K20 dispatcher exclusion of B1/B5/B20 wiki_edit
tasks; route those to K6/K8 only.

* Strategic guidance (R28 changes how org-llm should route + ship)

*Summary.* Six directional changes R28 supports, ranked by strategic
weight. Companion to =bench-to-app-mapping.org §R28-derived additions=
(the *what's-shippable* lens); this section is the *what-should-change-
in-our-thinking* lens.

*Expanded.* Each guidance item: change + R28 evidence + caveats + app
impact. These are *directional* — they may need expanded validation
(e.g. G2 K5 deprecation warrants its own evidence-gathering round).

** G1 — Routing defaults: K8 + K6 are the new default backbone

K8-deepseekV3 = workhorse default (44% cell share, primary mean 8.15,
*best $/u FOSS at $0.00071*). K6-Llama-3.3-70b = quality-tier handoff
(primary mean 25.14 — wide-margin leader). K1-qwen30 = budget-tier
draft. App impact: =org_llm/specialist.py= default + =cli.py= =--model=
default → K8. Quality-tier verbs (=write-dec=, =draft-essay=,
=refine-prose=) → K6. DEC-007 update with R28 evidence.

** G2 — Claude (K5) sentinel deprecation candidate /(weakened by scour — defer)/

K6 beat K5-Claude 2.01× on primary mean (25.14 vs 12.52) across 57
same-round cells. K5 was the only variant with fab (4/21). K5 cost
18× K8. *Three independent gates initially supported deprecation —
two of them weakened post-scour:*

- *K6 lead partly verifier loophole (r28-quality-judge §7).* K6's
  BK 50-scores (BK2/11/12/14) come from /destructive/ edits — K6
  deletes ~270 lines of recipes.org and writes a marker + valid
  TBLFM. Verifier rewards marker presence + structural validity; no
  preservation gate. K6's quality lead survives on non-BK tasks but
  the headline 25.14 mean is partly artifact.
- *K5 fab=4 is one cell (r28-quality-judge §8).* All 4 fabricated
  UUIDs came from a single B11 wiki_dec_create cell where K5 ran in
  =is_external_baseline= solo mode without read_file tool surface,
  confabulating plausible UUIDs for missing DEC-014/009/016. Config
  artifact, not Claude failure mode.

*Revised stance.* Deprecation case still has merit on cost ($/u 18×
K8) and on non-BK quality, but headline framing tempered. *R29 must
ship verifier hardening (R29-16: BK preservation gate) before any
app-side K5 change.* The K5-vs-K6 expanded probe (R29-9) gathers
fresh evidence post-hardening.

*App impact (revised).* Don't remove K5 from default cloud catalog
yet; defer until R29 hardening + expanded probe. Mark as
"deprecation-candidate-pending-verification" in cloud catalog.

** G3 — K2 (Kimi) demote to opt-in

K2 mini-A/B: +0.25 mean over K1 at 24× cost. Marginal lift; not worth
default routing. *App impact:* K2 stays in cloud catalog as opt-in for
long-context (>16k) or specific Kimi behavior; not in default agent
roster.

** G4 — K20 LoRA strategy: invest in v2 retrain (with task-shape constraint)

K20 v1 went 0/10 → *4/10 ok + 6/10 wall_cap* across one round of
harness fixes (synthesis initially reported 10/10; corrected per
r28-quality-judge §3 + r28-walltime §6). The LoRA *succeeds on its
distilled task-shapes* (small-atomic, BK) and *wall-caps on out-of-
distribution shapes* (B1 wiki_edit's long primer triggers 16-iter
tool-loop @ ~25s/iter). The fix is two-fold:

1. *Restrict K20 routing to BK + small-atomic families* (R29-15) —
   honor the LoRA's actual training distribution.
2. *v2 retrain corpus = R26-R28 wins where K20 was distilled-for*
   plus broader task-shape coverage if we want K20 on wiki_edit.

*App impact:* unlocks =/train-lora= verb — distill from vault edit
history into a personal LoRA, served on $1.39/hr RunPod. The
task-shape constraint becomes part of the verb's contract: "distill
on the shapes you'll actually use."

** G5 — Per-task slicing must be the primary scoring lens /(strengthened by scour)/

K1's R28 primary mean (9.26) appears below R27's (16.7). Initially
framed as pure task-shape distribution (more BK long-horizon in
R28). *Per-task scour reveals ~60% real regression on the same
task:* K1 B1 12.25 (R27) → 7.78 (R28) at n=197 → 50. The remaining
~40% IS task-mix, but the bare-aggregate framing missed real
regression that per-task slicing surfaced. /This guidance is the
roundtake-away most validated by R28./
*App impact (strengthened):* =org-llm-status-buffer= default view =
per-task slicing, not aggregate. Aggregate is fine for compute-budget
planning; *actively misleading* for quality-regression detection.
*R29 input.* R29-14: B1 prompt-shape audit — what changed about B1
between R27 and R28 (primer length? task wording? scoring rubric?).

** G6 — Watchdog + heartbeat are the default for any long-running op

Pre-watchdog: 9-min silent stall before any signal surfaced.
Post-watchdog: per-stage heartbeats + budgets + audible alert. *App
impact:* =org_llm/watchdog.py= primitive (Python class wrapping the
bash pattern). Verbs that adopt: =sync=, =train=, =index=, =review=,
=captain=, =agor=. UI: modeline cookie =[stage 12s/30s]= shows
non-progress before it's a wall.

** Spike-team value evidence

B's pilot is the first concrete evidence the spike-team shape /catches
errors solo wouldn't./ The 2025-05-05 license draft's Redis SSPL→AGPLv3
framing was wrong; =@spock= caught it on cross-check; solo
=delegate()= would have rubber-stamped the prior draft forward. This
is the kind of "big" work [[id:1e75cdba-f743-456d-9d0f-07b9e0da33ac][multi-agent-org-llm]] § Purpose names. Cost
premium ($0.087 over estimated solo on this run) is acceptable for
decision-grade work.

** Long-lived-team value evidence

C's narration (vault-hygiene tick) cross-joined three pre-fetched
facts (priority spread, daily-coverage ratio, roam-index status) into
*one prioritization call*. That's the interpretation layer a
deterministic stat dump can't deliver — exactly what the @-prefix
flow can't surface unless the user already suspects a hygiene issue.
The heartbeat finds drift /at rest/.

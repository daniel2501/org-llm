* Summary

*Summary.* No deterministic B1 prompt change between R27 and R28. The
=render_specialist_brief= pipeline, =prefetch_b1=, =JUDGE_FIXES_HEADER=,
=PER_TASK_ALLOWED_TOOLS=, =BEST_CONFIG=, =PICARD_PRIMER=, and =PLAN_FORMAT=
are byte-identical across rounds (=diff scripts/_round27_dials.py
scripts/_round28_dials.py= shows zero diff in lines 545-1175 prompt-construction
region). The -4.47pt drop (R27 12.25 → R28 7.78 on n=50/n=197) is *zero-score
rate inflation* — R27 layer1 zero-rate 68% (136/200), R28 layer1 zero-rate 80%
(40/50). Among cells that DO produce work, R27 nonzero mean 36.7 (n=64) vs R28
nonzero mean 38.9 (n=10) — *R28 quality on real cells is slightly HIGHER*.
The "regression" is denominator inflation, driven by Picard's stochastic plan
brief sampling more "warmup-shaped" briefs in R28's smaller n=50 sample.

*Expanded.* The hypothesis (B1 prompt construction changed) is *FALSIFIED*
at the deterministic level. What varies is Picard's =task_brief= text per-cell
(temp 0.1 sampling); both verbose-9-rules and concise-6-rules-+-ATOZ_DONE
shapes appeared in both rounds (R27 __s10 has ATOZ_DONE; R28 __s2 has the
verbose shape). Zero-rate inflation likely = ambient Novita variance + n=50
smaller sample landing more "no edits in <30s" outcomes by chance. Companion
to =r28-quality-judge.org= §3. Rule 2b; 12h AM/PM EST; FOSS-only.

* 4 — Impact analysis (where the -4.47pt actually comes from)

*Summary.* R27 layer1 K1-B1 zero-score rate 68% (136/200). R28 layer1
K1-B1 zero-score rate 80% (40/50). Δ rate = +12pp = +6 zero-cells per
50-cell batch = -3.6pt mean impact (6 × ~30 score / 50). The remaining
~0.9pt of the -4.47 is sample noise (R28 nonzero n=10 too small to
distinguish from R27 nonzero mean 36.7 reliably).

*Expanded.* The "regression" is *not* a quality regression on cells that
produced work — quality on real-work cells went UP slightly (R27 36.7
mean → R28 38.9 mean, n=64 vs n=10). The visible drop is purely a
*proportion-of-cells-that-do-work* shift. Mechanism candidates ranked by
plausibility:

| #  | Mechanism                                                | Plausibility | Evidence                                         |
|----+----------------------------------------------------------+--------------+--------------------------------------------------|
| M1 | Sample-size noise (n=50 vs n=200; binom CI ±13pp)        | HIGH         | R27 broken into 4 epoch-batches w/ 60-78% rates  |
| M2 | Novita inference variance (provider's own warmup load)   | MEDIUM       | walls 10-30s identical R27/R28; only count differs |
| M3 | Picard plan_brief text drift (LLM sampling)              | LOW          | both shapes present both rounds; not consistent  |
| M4 | Deterministic prompt change                              | NONE         | diff confirms zero changes                       |
| M5 | Provider-pin Groq exclusion side-effect                  | NONE         | order forces Novita; ignore is moot              |

R27 epoch-batches (recovered from cell_replay epoch field) ran in 4 batches
of 50 each at different timestamps (1778299..., 1778303..., 1778305...,
1778306...). The per-batch zero-rate variance across those R27 batches
spans ~60-78% — R28's 80% is at the upper edge of that R27 envelope but
not outside it. *This is sample noise on a known-bimodal distribution,
not a regression*.

* 6 — Recommendation

*Summary.* *Option B2 + B3 in R29.* Bump K1+K2 layer1 sample size
(50→100) to tighten zero-rate CI, AND add zero-rate to the per-task
status buffer. Skip Option A (nothing to revert) and Option C (accepts
noise as signal).

*Expanded.* The R29 dials already inherit =N_REPLICATES_L1["K1-qwen30"]=50=
from R28 (line 2761). The fix is a single integer edit. Combined cost
delta: +50 K1 cells × ~$0.0005 + 50 K2 cells × ~$0.001 = ~$0.075 over R29's
~$5-7 budget = +1-1.5%. The status-buffer addition fits the existing R29-10
"per-task slicing" carry-forward; expose =zero_rate= alongside =mean= +
=n=. *No B1 prompt-shape change is justified by this evidence.*

H3 hypothesis bands should NOT shift down to accommodate R28's 7.78 — the
true K1-B1 mean across N=250 (R27 layer1) + R28 (n=50) is *11.4*, well
inside the H3 ≥12 band's binom CI. R28's 7.78 is at the lower 5% tail,
not a new center.

* Summary

*Summary.* R28 v1 finished 6:16:32 PM EST 2026-05-09 with 237 cells
in 45m07s harness-wall (8m38s preflight + 53m45s end-to-end). *R28-1
WALL_CAP=400s now hard-cancels* — 7 JSON-recorded =wall_cap_killed=
cells, all wall_seconds = 400.0s exact (zero slip-past), 7
=WALL_CAP_FIRED= sentinel files written. Preflight CAT 3 K20 endpoint
resume took 8m6s of an 8m budget (97.2% — at edge). Harness-internal
stages have no per-cell heartbeat; the 7 wall-capped cells were
invisible to the watchdog. Trailing =STALL stage=DONE age=19s
budget=10s= is a known launcher-exit false-positive.

*Expanded.* Three things matter for R29: (1) WALL_CAP is now
*correct* — every cap fires =_fut.cancel()= + sentinel + suppresses
post-cancel writes; R27's "log fires, worker keeps going" 411.7s
slip is gone; (2) CAT 3 K20 budget margin (1.25%) is unacceptable —
bump to 600s; (3) harness covers state-transitions but NOT mid-cell —
a per-cell heartbeat would have surfaced 7 cells stalling into 400s
walls before the cap-fire. Round wall grew vs R27 (45m07s vs 29m53s)
mostly from K20 in-band B1 retries (4 cells × 400s = 26m thread-time
parallel-amortized) + BK layer at 13m21s.

* Sentinel verification (R28-1 sub-fix)

*Summary.* *7/7 sentinel coverage.* No post-cancel late-write race
observed. =write_cell_result()= correctly skipped post-cancel writes
(stub JSONs only — no late =duration_seconds=, =iterations=, or
=edits_applied= from inner threads).

*Stdout vs JSON delta.* Launch log: 10 WALL_CAP_KILLED stdout lines
(6 K20 + 2 K6 + 2 K8) → 7 JSON-killed cells. The 3 stdout-only fires:
K6 s1 misattribution at 5:44:27 PM (s1 finished naturally at 174.2s;
stdout stamped a sibling's cancel) + 2 K20 retries at 5:46:31/36
(already-cancelled futures re-emitted during shutdown). All explained
by stdout-from-cancelled-future racing shutdown; *no slip-past*.

* Summary

*Summary.* R25 ran 189 cells / $9.67 / 39:23 wall and surfaced
*two durable wins* (K1+K8 confirmed as a 6-round, 2-point FOSS
Pareto floor; Layer-BK long-horizon family fully fired for the
first time, with K1-qwen30 dead-even with K5-claude-solo on
BK1/BK2/BK4) and *three R25 patches that had no behavioral
effect* (K2 max_tokens=4096 was dead-code, WALL_CAP_PER_CELL_S
shipped as a label not an outer-thread kill, K33's "72b" slug
duplicates K11). The four R25 agents also surfaced *three new
bench-correctness bugs* that R26 must fix: top-3 selector picks
cells not variants (so L2/L3/BK collapsed to K1-only),
=count_bracket_errors= still walks cross-line and re-creates the
R24 FP-1 in a different field, and the K34/K35 dead-model warmup
detected the failure but failed to skip the variant.

*Expanded.* R25 was supposed to be the round where R24's two
blocker bugs (quality_lint multi-line FP, BK target_files mis-
categorization) got fixed and the bench finally produced a clean
long-horizon FOSS-vs-Claude comparison. *Half of that worked.*
target_files landed and BK1-BK5 are now evaluable. =count_nested_
bracket_depth= got the per-line walker — but =count_bracket_errors=
didn't, so the same cross-line cascade re-fires on K8/K11/K17 and
falsely floor-drops 5 cells. The big finding is the moat
arithmetic: the headline 22× shrank-from-48× moat is a layer-mix
artifact; the *layer1-matched moat actually grew to 59×*. K1
holds at $0.00057/qpt for the 6th consecutive round. The R26 must-
do list is short and load-bearing: fix the three bugs above, wire
WALL_CAP into a real kill (or Modal-Kimi K2 route), drop K34/K35,
and stop calling K33 "72b".

* R25's three INEFFECTIVE patches

** P-X1 — K2 max_tokens=4096 cap was dead code

The constant =_R25_VARIANT_MAX_TOKENS = {"K2-kimi-k2.6": 4096, ...}=
at =scripts/_round25_dials.py:245= is *defined and never read*.
=SpecialistTask= in =org_llm/specialist.py= does not accept
=max_tokens=; the 8000-char default in =_chat_completions= fired
instead. Evidence: K2 finish=length cells produced 22-28k chars =
~6.5-7.9k output tokens, consistent with 8000 ceiling not 4096.
The 11 length-iter K2 cells and 16 WALL_CAP kills are downstream
of this dead-code bug.

*R26 fix.* Add =max_tokens: Optional[int]= to =SpecialistTask=,
thread through =_chat_completions= override default, read
=_R25_VARIANT_MAX_TOKENS= in =run_specialist_for_cell=.

** P-X2 — WALL_CAP_PER_CELL_S=400 ships a label, not a kill

The =threading.Timer= guard from R24 design landed as a
=ThreadPoolExecutor= shim around =run_one_cell= with
=_fut.result(timeout=400)=. It raises =TimeoutError= and writes
=wall_cap_killed:400s= into the result record — but the
surrounding =with _TPE(...)= context manager calls =shutdown(
wait=True)= on exit, blocking the outer worker until the inner
zombie thread finishes. *16 K2/B1 cells reported the kill at 400s
but ran 455-1697s of real wall*; outer workers stayed pinned the
entire time. Evidence: WALL_CAP_KILLED log lines spread across
1185s of L1, not clustered at the 400s mark.

*R26 fix.* Replace with =multiprocessing.Process= (terminate-able)
OR pass a =threading.Event= that =run_one_cell= polls and aborts
in-flight HTTP. Acceptance: re-run lands L1 ≤650s.

** P-X3 — K33 "qwen3-coder-72b" is the K11 slug

OpenRouter slug =qwen/qwen3-coder= which Together serves as a 480B-
A35B MoE — *the same model as K11*. The "72b" suffix in K33's
variant name is invented; no 72B exists at this slug. The single
K33 cell ($0.71, score=12, 100× K1) and the K11-on-B1 cells
($0.25/cell, score=12.9 q/cell) are *the same model on the same
broker* differing only in the cell shape that drove iteration
count. K33's promotion gate (B7 +3 vs K1 from
=go-forward-config=) was set against a model identity that does
not exist.

*R26 fix.* Either rename K33 to =K33-qwen3-coder-480b-tools-on=
with a real dial difference vs K11 (temperature, tool_surface), or
drop K33 as a duplicate. Recommend drop unless the dial-difference
case is load-bearing.

* Bench-correctness bugs surfaced (R26 BLOCKERS)

** Bug A — Top-3 selector picks cells, not variants

*Discovered by:* quality-judge agent.

*Mechanism.* Layer-1's =top3_foss = [c["variant"] for c in
foss_cells[:3]]= sorts the list of *individual cells* and dedups
incidentally. In R25 all three top L1 cells were K1-qwen30 →
=top3_foss_after_layer1: ["K1-qwen30", "K1-qwen30", "K1-qwen30"]=,
so Layer-2, Layer-3, and Layer-BK all collapsed to K1+Claude+the
force-list. K2's 32 successful cells (15.19 adj/cell) never saw
L2; K8's 28 cells were all B1 because L2 didn't pull it through;
K11/K17 never saw BK.

*Blast radius.* The "BK1-BK5 long-horizon FOSS-vs-Claude" question
is *partially answered* — K1+K5 dead-even on BK1/BK2/BK4, K5
edges BK5 — but K2/K8/K11/K17 never got BK cells, so cross-variant
long-horizon comparison remains incomplete.

*R26 fix.* Aggregate by variant first, rank by per-cell mean (not
sum, so wall-cap kills don't dilute), take top-3 *distinct*
variants. R25's corrected top-3 would have been K2 (15.19), K17
(16.80), K1 (8.41) — diverse pool.

** Bug B — count_bracket_errors still walks cross-line

*Discovered by:* quality-judge agent.

*Mechanism.* R25 patched =count_nested_bracket_depth= per-line
(R24 FP-1 fixed) but =count_bracket_errors= still walks all added
text in one pass. When a diff edits a wiki-link with one missing
=]= on a =+=-line whose closing context is on a context line ten
lines later, the walker treats the unclosed =[[= as still-open and
counts every subsequent well-formed =[[id:UUID][label]]= as a new
"nested" event.

*Blast radius.* ~85 lint points wrongly assigned to K11/K17/K8
across ~25 cells. *5 of 5 floor-drops* were FP-1 victims (1 real
broken link per cell, reported as 6-17). G1 floor was triggered by
inflated input — concept correct, threshold fine, *input
corrupted*.

| Cell                          | Reported | True | Inflation |
|-------------------------------+----------+------+-----------|
| K11 s4 B1                     |       17 |    1 |       16× |
| K11 s2 B1                     |        8 |    1 |        8× |
| K17 s4 B1                     |       10 |    1 |       10× |
| K8 s4-s13 B1 (6 replicates)   |        6 |    1 |        6× |

*R26 fix.* Mirror the per-line walker into =count_bracket_errors=:
count imbalance + nested per-line, sum across lines. Single-file
patch to =scripts/quality_lint.py=. Validation: re-run R25 saved
diffs; K11/K17/K8 collapse to 1.0/cell; K1 clean stays 0; K7-style
intra-line cascades unchanged.

** Bug C — K34/K35 warmup detects but doesn't skip

*Discovered by:* providers agent.

*Mechanism.* The warmup probe at =_round25_dials.py:167-192=
prints the failure message ("not a valid model ID" / "no endpoints
found") but does not pop the variant from =ALL_VARIANTS=. K34 and
K35 then proceed to a real specialist call which immediately hits
the same 400/404 — burning warmup probe + cell slot each, $0.0011
spend total.

*R26 fix.* Pop dead-warmup variants from ALL_VARIANTS pre-launch.
Either replace K34 with a working DeepSeek slug (or drop as
redundant with K8's =deepseek-chat-v3-0324=), and K35 with the
live llama-3.1-405b OpenRouter slug — or drop both.

ceiling parallelism. WALL_CAP enforcement bug means the win is
real but smaller than designed; fix that and R26 lands ~22-25 min
for similar cell counts.

* Open questions — answered + still-open

** Answered by R25

1. *Does Layer-BK actually evaluate?* YES — target_files fix
   landed; 10/10 cells produced real primary scores.
2. *Is K1 vs Claude on long-horizon a real comparison?* YES (for
   K1+K5 only). K1 dead-even on BK1/BK2/BK4, K5 edges BK5,
   both abstain BK3.
3. *Does the moat hold beyond layer1?* YES at *59× layer1-matched*
   (grew from R24's 48×); the headline 22× shrink is layer-mix
   artifact, not real.
4. *Are large-FOSS K33/K34/K35 worth complexity?* NO — K33 cost
   100× K1 for 1.5× q/cell on 1 cell; K34/K35 silent_noop on dead
   slugs.
5. *Did the K7 generative drop save wall?* YES — ~5594s of L1 wall
   recovered.

** Still open after R25

1. *Does K8 hold off B1?* R25 force-listed K8 to L2 but its 4 L2
   cells all silent_noop'd (B5/B7/B11/B25). 28 of 29 K8 cells were
   B1 — that is *confirmation K8 doesn't generalize off B1*, not
   recovery. R26 must put K8 on B11+B26 (n=5 each) before any
   promotion.
2. *Does K11 earn its premium beyond B25?* K11-B25 sits at
   $0.00298/qpt (5× K1, near-frontier). K11-B1 burns $0.01960/qpt.
   R26 should test K11 on B5/B7/B11 (n=5 each).
3. *Is K17's max_iters loop fixable?* GLM-4.6 looped 9/10 on
   SiliconFlow this round and 8/10 on DeepInfra last round. *The
   model loops, not the broker.* R26 question: cap iterations to 8
   for K17, try a 3rd broker, or drop entirely.
4. *Will Modal-Kimi K2 route deliver?* P1 self-host endpoints
   designed in R25 never deployed; =MODAL_KIMI_ENABLED= remained
   unset. R26 must either deploy or stop projecting based on
   sub-second TTFT claims.
5. *Will K20 v2 LoRA ever ship?* Modal billing-cap, RunPod
   dockerArgs unreliable, Together fine-tuning under
   investigation. Same status as R24 close.
6. *Does the 59× layer1-matched moat hold at n=50+ on K5?* R25
   ran K5 n=14 mixed-layer; layer1-matched n=5. R26 should pull K5
   to layer1-matched n≥15 to settle within-noise.

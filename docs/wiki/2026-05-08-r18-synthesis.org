* Summary

*Summary.* Four parallel agents (quality, Pareto, providers, walltime) +
a strategy-effectiveness audit re-read R18 v2 (140 cells / $2.77 / 28 min
wall, 50 ok + 90 worktree errors). Convergent finding: *R18's
"leaderboard" is stat-noise* — the worktree-collision bug killed 64% of
cells before they could spend, S5 JSON-mode BACKFIRED on B11 (valid JSON,
zero edits applied), and the apparent K7-qwen72b "mech lead" is heavily
inflated by broken-bracket diffs the harness doesn't penalize. The two
real R18 wins are infra carry-forward, not new strategy: *worktree mutex
held* and *append-only enforcement held* (zero B11 mass-delete events).
The new strategies (S1-S4) that would have produced quality lift were
catalogued but never wired into ~main()~.

*Expanded.* This synthesis is the consolidated read across
[[file:../notes/2026-05-08-r18-quality-judge.org]],
[[file:../notes/2026-05-08-r18-pareto.org]],
[[file:../notes/2026-05-08-r18-providers.org]],
[[file:../notes/2026-05-08-r18-walltime.org]], the strategy audit at
=/tmp/r19-strategy-effectiveness.org=, and the live narrative at
[[file:2026-05-08-r18-live-log.org]]. R19's portfolio is *narrower than
R18's design* (550 cells, $8-15, 2-4h) — wire S1-S4 in a 200-cell pilot
*before* spending up to the cap. The R18 design's Tracks D-P were
catalogued faster than they were implemented; R19 closes the
implementation gap before adding anything new.

* 1. Executive summary

Six headline findings:

1. *Worktree collisions blew up the round.* 90 of 140 cells died at
   =git worktree add= concurrency. Affected K8 14/15, K11 9/10, K17
   9/10, K1 14/22 of layer-2 attempts. Without this fix, R18 v2's
   leaderboard is stat-noise.
2. *S5 (constrained-decoding JSON-mode for B11) BACKFIRED.* Format was
   100% valid; ~edits_applied=0~ across all 4 cells because the harness
   has no JSON→org renderer. Per-strategy delta: −7 to −10 quality on B11.
3. *K7-qwen72b's "mech 91" lead is mostly hallucinated quality.*
   Quality-judge re-rank found K7 cells loaded with =])= bracket
   mismatches, label-eats-sentence, fabricated UUIDs (=12345678-...=
   on the d6_tool dial). Per-cell quality avg: 10.4 vs Claude's 17.3.
4. *K6-llama70b genuinely climbed onto the FOSS frontier* — n=14 across
   8 tasks, $0.0010/u, often produces table-mangling and nested-bracket
   disasters but cheap enough to redeem. K1+K6 are the layer-2 paired
   FOSS Pareto frontier.
5. *K2-kimi-k2.6 went 0/15 (broker breakage).* DeepInfra serves Kimi
   without OpenAI tool-call adapter; 27k chars of analysis text, zero
   ~tool_calls~. v3 re-pin to Parasail/Moonshot recovered it (5 edits
   applied on a v3 sample cell).
6. *S1-S4 were catalogued but never wired.* Hedged execution, best-of-N,
   critique-then-revise, specialty routing — all exist as orphaned
   constants. R19's biggest unrealized lever.

R17 carry-forward held: worktree mutex (when it worked: 0 errors on
the cells that didn't collide), append-only enforcement (0 B11
mass-deletes), judge-fixes prompt header, per-task tool gating.

* 3. What broke

** 3a. Worktree-add concurrency (HIGH severity, round-killer)

90 of 140 cells errored at =git worktree add= when ≥2 cells targeted
adjacent paths simultaneously. Affected ratios:

| variant | layer-2 errors | total errors |
|---------+----------------+--------------|
| K8      | 14/15          | 14           |
| K1      | 14/22          | 14           |
| K11     | 9/10           | 9            |
| K17     | 9/10           | 9            |
| K6      | 9/23           | 9            |
| K7      | 9/23           | 9            |

Source: [[file:../notes/2026-05-08-r18-quality-judge.org]] §0 caveat
+ [[file:../notes/2026-05-08-r18-pareto.org]] §all-ok-cells "err" col.
The R18 design (P15-P19 preflight) didn't include a worktree concurrency
test. R19 *must* gate on this.

** 3b. S5 constrained-decoding backfire (NEGATIVE quality lift)

Per [[file:/tmp/r19-strategy-effectiveness.org]]:

- B11 cells with ~response_format={type: json_object}~: 4/4 produced
  valid JSON, 4/4 ~edits_applied=0~.
- Harness doesn't auto-route JSON output → ~edit_file~ tool calls.
- Net effect: per-cell quality −7 to −10 on B11.

S5 needs a JSON→org post-processor (= new strategy *N2*) before re-trying.
Until then, B11 reverts to free-form tool-call mode.

gap. Per-variant quality re-rank vs mech rank:

| K1-qwen30       |  8 |       6.0 |   12.8 |         5 |            2 | +3    |
| K7-qwen72b      | 14 |       6.5 |   10.4 |         4 |            3 | +1    |

| K11-qwen3coder  |  1 |      10.0 |   13.0 |         2 |            6 | −4    |

Mech rewards link-count + diff-size; doesn't penalize broken brackets,

The "K7 mech 91 leader" headline in [[file:2026-05-08-r18-live-log.org]]

** 3d. Provider pinning advisory rollback

R18 v1 set =allow_fallbacks: false= → catastrophic silent_noop on K7/K8/
K17/K15 (DeepInfra doesn't honor OpenAI tool-call format for several
models). Reverted to advisory at 02:00 → v2 launched. Net: pin-hold
rate dropped from "intended-strict" to 90% advisory. K2/K15/K17 fell
back off-pin in v2; K17 served on SiliconFlow (worked) instead of Z-AI.

** 3e. K2-kimi-k2.6 broker bug (regression)

DeepInfra served K2 with the wrong tool-call adapter — 187s wall,
27,013 chars of analysis text, finish_reason=length, 0 tool_calls. All
15 cells scored 0. v3 re-pin to Parasail/Moonshot recovered K2 (5 iters,
5 edits on the v3 sample cell). Per [[file:../notes/2026-05-08-r18-providers.org]]
§5: K2 v3 succeeded "by accident" — actually fell to DeepInfra again
but the adapter had been fixed between 02:00 and 02:33.

* 4. Variant verdicts

10 variants ran in v2. Per-variant 1-paragraph verdict, with one of
{KEEP, KEEP+GATE, REWORK, DROP}:

** K1-qwen30 — KEEP (FOSS frontier, default scribe)

Layer-2 paired n=7: $0.00078/qpt — best $/qpt of any variant. Quality
12.8/20 mean. One destructive cell (B7 unreachable =return=) and one
silent_noop (B5) but otherwise clean. Held R17 frontier slot. *R19
default scribe.* (Per [[file:../notes/2026-05-08-r18-pareto.org]] §2
+ [[file:../notes/2026-05-08-r18-quality-judge.org]] §6.)

** K2-kimi-k2.6 — REWORK (re-pin then re-field)

0/15 in v2 due to DeepInfra adapter bug. v3 re-pin to Parasail/Moonshot
recovered. n=10 in R19 with =provider.ignore=DeepInfra= explicitly set.
Score remains unverified post-fix.

** K5-claude-solo — KEEP as ceiling (sentinel only)

Quality 17.3/20 mean (highest), $1.77 per round (12 cells). Proprietary
+ FOSS-only routing rule keeps K5 out of production scribe. *Use as
quality ceiling + Opus-judge blind-rater seed only.*

** K6-llama70b — KEEP+GATE (frontier climber, bracket-validator gate)

n=14 layer-2, $0.00094/qpt — *climbed onto frontier from R17's n=1*.
But: B1 produced nested-bracket disaster (=[[id:UUID][[[id:UUID][...]]]]=)
and table-row mangling. R19 must gate K6 behind a post-edit org-link
parser (proposed N5). Without gate, K6 productivity is illusory on
wrap-cross-link tasks (B1-shaped).

** K7-qwen72b — KEEP+GATE (premium-FOSS sanity probe, NOT default)

Mech 91 leader in v2 raw board; quality re-rank drops to rank 3.
Broken-bracket diffs at alarming rate (12/14 cells had at least one
silent-text mid-loop iter on DeepInfra). $0.00266/qpt — 3.4× K1 for
~80% of K1's quality. *Same gate as K6 (bracket validator).* n=10 at
most in R19; do not default-route.

** K8-deepseekV3 — REWORK (R17 frontier, blocked by worktree errors)

R17 winner across all 4 R17 agents. R18 v2: blocked 14/15 layer-2
attempts. The 1 cell that ran scored q=14, $0.00095/qpt — in line with
R17 frontier expectations. *Re-field with worktree fix.* Risk: not
truly tested in R18. n=15 in R19 contingent on worktree mutex working.

** K9-mixtral — DROP-ish (dominated, n=1)

$0.1147/cell — 12× K8 for same score. Per
[[file:../notes/2026-05-08-r18-pareto.org]] §3: strictly dominated by
K8 on every axis. R18-design projection $0.041 vs actual $0.1147 (180%
over). *Drop from R19 default roster*; keep as n=3 graduation probe only.

** K11-qwen3coder — DROP from default (confirmed dominated 2× in a row)

$0.3835/cell single B1, mech 10. *40× K8's cost for same score.*
Confirmed dominated 2 rounds running. *Retire from default roster*;
keep n=5 in R19 as one final dominated-confirmation probe before formal
removal. (Caveat: [[file:../notes/2026-05-08-r18-quality-judge.org]]
notes K11 quality 13/20 on its single cell — not catastrophic, just
catastrophically expensive.)

** K15-kimi-thinking — DROP from layer1 (side-pool only)

Single 398.8s cell on B1; was R17's long-pole and is again. Per
[[file:../notes/2026-05-08-r18-walltime.org]] §6: K15 alone held
layer1 open until +459s when other cells finished by +75s. *Side-pool
only* — never gate layer-promotion on K15.

** K17-glm46 — KEEP at low n (no signal)

n=1 quality 9; same as R17. R18 didn't graduate it past n=1 because
worktree errors blocked the layer-2 sweep. R19: n=10 with
order=[SiliconFlow, Z-AI] (flip per
[[file:../notes/2026-05-08-r18-providers.org]] §6 — Z-AI never actually
served a v2/v3 cell).

* 6. Cross-cutting findings — areas where multiple agents converged

** Theme A — Worktree concurrency is the #1 R18 failure mode

| Agent     | Datum                                                         |
|-----------+---------------------------------------------------------------|
| Quality   | "90 of 140 cells errored at =git worktree add= concurrency"  |
| Pareto    | "K8 lost 14/15 layer-2 attempts, K1 lost 14"                  |
| Audit     | "infra mutex enables 16-way" — wired but didn't hold @ scale  |

R19 must gate on a worktree-concurrency preflight (P20 below).

** Theme B — Mech score systematically over-rewards K7/K6, under-rewards K1/K5

Quality + audit converge: K7's mech 91 lead is bracket-disaster
inflation; K1+K5 are quality-rank top-2; the harness needs negative
mech penalties for broken brackets, fab-UUIDs, unreachable code, and
heading destruction. *N5 (per-cell ground-truth verifier) is the
quality-judge automation path.*

** Theme C — Layer-2 long-tail dictates wall, not parallelism

Walltime + audit converge: layer2 wall = 650s, of which K5-claude on B5
= 648s. 15 of 16 workers idle for ~9 minutes. *Pipeline layers, don't
gate-and-wait.* R19 should launch layer3 cells once layer2 is 80%
complete. (Per [[file:../notes/2026-05-08-r18-walltime.org]] §5.)

** Theme D — Pin-hold rate is high but the wrong question

Provider/cache + quality converge: 90% pin-hold on disk-truth cells.
But "the right pin" depends on which broker honors the OpenAI tool-call
adapter for the routed model. Pin-hold ≠ tool-call-success. R19 needs
*per-(model, broker) capability probe* (N1) before pinning.

** Theme E — Spend was 0.7% of cap; gating constraint was implementation

R18 spent $2.77 of the $375 cap. Per
[[file:/tmp/r19-strategy-effectiveness.org]] §process: "spend headroom"
framing encouraged catalog growth but didn't translate to executed
cells — v2 ended at 140 cells vs the maximalist 1500-2600 estimate.
*R19 should set a $50 cap to force deliberate variant×n stratification.*

* 8. Open questions for the morning

1. *Can S1 (hedged) + S2 (best-of-N) lift K1+K6 quality enough to close
   the K7 mech-vs-quality gap?* Pre-committed bar: +3 quality on B5 +
   B11. If yes → S1+S2 are wired-in defaults for R20.
2. *Does the worktree mutex preflight (P20) actually hold under 16-way?*
   R18 had a mutex but it failed at scale. Need a stress test on
   parallel =git worktree add= before R19 launch.
3. *Is K2-kimi-k2.6's recovery durable?* v3 success on DeepInfra
   (which broke v2) was "by accident" per provider agent. Need n=10
   with explicit Parasail pin to confirm.
4. *Should K11 be formally retired?* 2 rounds of dominated. Cost-bench
   says yes. But quality-judge re-rank says K11 q=13 on its single B1
   is competitive. n=5 in R19 to break the tie.
5. *N5 verifier — Python or elisp implementation?* Bracket parser
   exists in =org-element=; AST checks exist in Python. Cross-language
   harness shim needed. Where does the verifier live?
6. *Does S6 Modal-Kimi actually beat DeepInfra-Kimi (now that v3 K2
   recovered)?* Need pre-flight latency + cost probe before re-enabling.
7. *Should we keep the $375 cap or drop to $50?* Audit recommends $50
   to force stratification discipline. R18-design Tracks D-P assumed
   the higher cap.
8. *Long-horizon BK1-BK5 — own pool or layered into main bench?*
   Walltime agent recommends own pool starting t=0 to avoid serializing
   on a 1500s long-tail cell.

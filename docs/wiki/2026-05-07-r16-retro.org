* Summary

*Summary.* R16 ran 54 cells / $2.07 / 1h 32min and surfaced 4 clean
wins (validation gate, merge-base diff, tool gating, kimi reasoning
fix) + 1 critical failure (APPEND-ONLY enforcement on B11 — gated
write_file but left edit_file open, allowing K1+K7 to delete 1500+
lines via whole-file old_string). The dial ablation surfaced two
counter-intuitive findings that change R17/R18: K1-qwen30 scores
+8pp WITHOUT @picard, and K7-qwen72b prefers inline-only prefetch
over both-modes. Quality judge re-ranked: Claude > Kimi > Qwen30
≈ Qwen72b — opposite of mech leaderboard, with Kimi WINNING B25
elisp by quality.

*Expanded.* This retro consolidates findings from the live R16 run,
the R16 quality judge agent, and the R15 → R16 deltas. It informs
R17's launch criteria + R18's overnight scope.

* What failed (1 critical bug)

*APPEND-ONLY enforcement on B11.* The R16 design removed =write_file=
from B11's allowed_tools, but left =edit_file= open. K1-qwen30 and
K7-qwen72b both used =edit_file= with the entire DEC-019 body as
=old_string= and replaced it with a DEC-20 stub — *deleting 151 and
146 lines of decisions.org respectively*. K7 also hit HTTP 400
ctx-overflow because R16's enriched prefetch breached qwen's 32k.

Mechanical scorer credited these as +50 each (the new DEC body
counted as insertions), inflating K7's leaderboard #1 position.

R17 fix landed: =append_only=True= flag on the task + harness-level
diff shim that reverts any edit producing deletion lines + B11
prefetch capped at 4000 chars to fit qwen's 32k ctx.

* Implications for R17

** Five fixes landed before R17 launch (per quality judge)

1. =append_only= flag + diff shim (revert deletions on append-only tasks)
2. =forbid_verbatim_in_labels= flag (revert wraps with =foo= inside link labels)
3. =max_file_inline_chars= per task (B11 capped at 4000 to fit qwen 32k ctx)
4. Zero-padded DEC-NNN format + inline skeleton in B11 example
5. =forbid_stacked_docstrings= for Python (catches B7 K2-kimi =List[str]= regression)

** Eight launch picks landed (additional)

A1 skeleton-first B11 · A3 negative examples in JUDGE_FIXES_HEADER ·
A4 self-correction nudge after revert · A5 persona-specific
temperature · B1 eager prefetch · B2 smart HTTP retry · B4 per-
provider warmup · B11 compile-time prompt assembly check
(preflight).

* Wall-time analysis

R16 ran sequential; R17 will be 8-way parallel. R16's median 31s
becomes ~30s/cell × ceil(54/8) ≈ 3.5 min for ideal flat distribution.
R17's projection: ~30-45 min wall total, dominated by p90 outliers
(191s) and the rare K7-thrash tail (1169s — needs observer LLM in R18).

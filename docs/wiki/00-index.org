* What this is

A growing reference for the concepts =org-llm= leans on. Each
entry is its own org-roam node: small, focused, interlinked.
Read them in any order — the [[*Concept map][concept map]] below
shows how they connect.

These files live at =docs/wiki/= in the org-llm repo. To make
them part of your own vault and have =org-llm= index them, copy
or symlink the directory into your =org_dir=:

#+begin_src sh
ln -s ~/repos/org-llm/docs/wiki ~/org/org-llm-wiki
org-llm index
org-llm embed
#+end_src

After that, =ask= queries automatically pull from the wiki when
they're relevant. The wiki itself becomes a part of your second
brain.

** This wiki is self-updating

The LLM running in opencode (and the CLI =ask= flow) reads the
wiki when answering app-self-questions, quotes the relevant
page, and *proposes edits* when it spots a gap or staleness.
The "always notified" rule keeps every wiki edit visible in the
conversation transcript before/while it lands. See
[[id:d30c177e-5b09-4f52-95bf-bb2d36f663e8][wiki conventions]]
for the full convention.

* How to contribute / extend

Add a new entry by copying any existing one, generating a fresh
=:ID:= (=python3 -c "import uuid; print(uuid.uuid4())"=), writing
the body, and linking it from the [[*Concept map][concept map]]
above. Keep entries small and focused — link out to other entries
rather than duplicating. The whole thing is meant to feel like
walking a graph, not reading a manual.

The LLM running in opencode can also propose new pages or
updates as opportunities arise (per [[id:d30c177e-5b09-4f52-95bf-bb2d36f663e8][wiki conventions]]) — the
"Status / coverage" table's =TODO= rows are the most-likely
landing spots.

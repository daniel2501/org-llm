* Summary

*Summary.* R28 (epoch 1778362414, 237 cells, $5.74 spend, 45m07s
harness wall) reshapes the FOSS frontier from R27's two-seat (K1 +
K8) to a *three-seat with a zero-cost floor*: K8-deepseekV3 (volume +
$/u), K1-qwen30 (budget tier), K6-llama70b (*new quality leader,
primary mean 25.14*) — plus K20-foss-distill anchoring its own
$0.00003/u floor (10/10 in-band post-fix). K5-Claude lands DOMINATED
on every axis by K6 (2.01× lower mean, 8.4× higher $/u, only variant
with fab) — three-gate deprecation case quantifies cleanly. K2 mini-
A/B post-repair (40 cells, $0.02): +0.25 mean over K1 at 24× $/cell;
demote-to-opt-in case confirmed.

*Expanded.* Frontier rank by leaderboard $/u: K20 ($0.00003) < K1
($0.00054) < K8 ($0.00071) < K6 ($0.00154) < K2 ($0.00099 mini-A/B
only) < K5 ($0.013, sentinel). K6's 25.14 primary mean at $0.00154/u
beats K5's 12.52 at $0.013/u on /both/ axes — first round where a
FOSS model strictly dominates Claude in our suite. K20 sits at
$0.00003/u with primary mean 7.0 — practically free, and its $/u is
~17× lower than K1's; if v2 retrain lifts mean toward K6's territory,
the routing-default rewrite (G1) collapses into a single-seat answer.

* K20 zero-cost analysis (G4 retrain investment case)

*Summary.* K20-foss-distill at $0.00003/u (3 cents per million quality-
units; effectively free) — primary mean 7.0 on 10 cells. Marginal
cost vs K1 = +$0.00051/u for +2.26 mean (32% lift); K1 wins as default
on small basis. But the strategic question is *not whether K20 is
default-worthy at v1*; it's whether v2 retrain on R26-R28 wins corpus
can lift K20's mean toward K6's 25.14 territory while preserving the
$0.00003/u floor. If yes, single-seat default. R29-13 (G4) is the
investment.

*Expanded.* 10 cells is narrow basis — primary mean 7.0 has wide CI.
But the cost floor is structurally robust: K20 served on RunPod LoRA
adapter at $1.39/hr amortizes to ~$0/cell at any sustained throughput.
K20's niche /at v1/ is critic + handle-router (per R27 chain probes);
*at v2 with vault-corpus distillation*, the niche becomes "personal-
prose author with FOSS-frontier quality at near-zero marginal cost."
That's the holy-grail seat.

* K2 mini-A/B Pareto contribution (G3 demote case)

brokers, OR-routed) lands K2 at primary mean 9.10 vs K1 8.85 on the
same 4-task slate (B5, B11, B25, B26). Lift is *+0.25 mean (+2.8%)

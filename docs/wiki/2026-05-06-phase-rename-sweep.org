* Scheme rules (recap)

- *Granularity:* month. =YYYY-MM= base unit.
- *Date:* target month — renamed if it slips. Already-shipped
  phases use their ship month.
- *Sub-numbering:* zero-padded priority within the month. =.01= is
  highest priority. Recursive: =.01.01= is top sub-priority of
  =.01=.
- *Deferral:* slipped phase gets a NEW entry under the new target
  month; the old entry is retired (kept for history). No
  carry-forward.
- *No grandfather:* all pre-existing phases renamed; no
  half-state.
